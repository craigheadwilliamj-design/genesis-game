# Guests, services, logistics, staff

Moved out of CLAUDE.md. Update this file (not CLAUDE.md) when the code it describes changes.

- `js/logistics.js`: food and medicine as goods. Medicine is three goods, one per era (`MED_GOODS` = `medpal`/`medmes`/`medcen`, `MED_GOOD[era]`, `medGoodOf(species)`), held in the PMC and cold stores' `b.store` and at the Botany Lab in `state.ceres.meds` ({medpal, medmes, medcen}; `cerMeds()`, `cerMedTotal()`, capped in total by `medCap()`); an animal is cured only by its own era's good. Contemporary medicine is a good of its own (`CMED` = `medmod`): it is on the standing orders (first ordered at `LOGI.cmedSeed` a night once a PMC exists, `MODERN.cost` a dose, billed as medicine), custodians carry it to the PMC, and vets use it when the era's medicine isn't on hand (`pickMed`). Old saves' `health.cmeds` counter is moved into the PMC's store by `upgradeSave`. `stockSummary` feeds the park office Logistics tab. `upgradeSave` splits an old single `meds` stock across the researched eras. Stores (`b.store`, `storeOf`, `addGood`/`takeGood`), spoilage, farms, work zones (`state.zones`, `e.zone`/`b.zone`/`k.zone`), and restock hauls (`pickHaul`, `supplyLines`). Medicine: custodians restock the PMC first and cold stores second (`pickHaul` sorts stations and the PMC before bulk stores), from docks, cold stores and the Botany Lab; medicine a worker is left holding (a leftover or a dropped haul, `returnCarry`/`returnCustCarry`) goes to `stashMed`: the PMC, then a cold store, then a dock (docks hold era medicine for this), and whatever doesn't fit is wasted.
  - Standing orders: one park-wide order a night per good not made in the park (`ORDER_GOODS`: animal food, guest goods, contemporary medicine), `state.logi.orders` (units a night; `orderOf`, `setOrder`, stepped by `orderStep`, capped by `orderMax`). The supply trucks come once a day at `LOGI.truckClock` (7 PM; `logiTick`, `state.logi.truckDay`): `dockDelivery` spreads each order over the reachable docks with the most room (animal food and medicine first, then guest goods share what's left). What doesn't fit (`logi.noRoom`) or can't be paid for (`logi.noCash`) isn't delivered or charged. A good with no order yet starts at what the park needs of it (`seedOrders`, `orderNeed`: food demand less farm output, `guestDemand`, average medicine use) the first time it needs any. `LOGI.bulk` takes a share off every unit of a big order (`unitPrice`, `orderPrice`; `basePrice` is before it, and partner parks pay for food while they still feed). `nightBill` is tonight's cost.
  - Use: `noteUse` counts what animals eat (`eatTick`, keepers.js), medicine the PMC uses (`useMeds`) and guest goods sold into `logi.used`; `logiNight` moves it to `usedLast` and the last `LOGI.histDays` nights into `logi.hist` (`usedAvg`).
  - Rush deliveries (`rushOrder(t)`, `rushLot`, `rushPrice`) land now at the dock with the most room, for `LOGI.rushMarkup` over `basePrice`, or `LOGI.expressMarkup` with the `express` tech. The `forecast` tech lets `state.logi.auto` set every order each night (`forecasting`, `forecastOrder`: a night's use plus half of what's missing from `LOGI.autoDays` of stock).
- `js/guests.js`: guest parties. Their needs (`NEEDS`), the guest path graph (`buildGuestGraph`, `guestField` per stop), `WIDE_PATH`/`isWide`/`node.room` (the old wide path, legacy only: no tool lays one and old ones become cells), deciding where to go (`planParty`), queues and service at food stands, shops and restrooms (`svcQ`, `serveParty`), mood, thoughts (`THOUGHTS`, `topThoughts`), and the nightly `guestsNight`. Each exhibit keeps a guest day in `e.glog` (`exLog(e)`, default in `upgradeSave`): `views` (guests who stopped to watch, counted in `seeExhibit`), `gifts` (donations from the leaving gift and donation boxes, split by `creditGifts` among the exhibits a party learned at, from `p.learnAt` filled by `learn(p, n, e)`) and `thoughts` (any thought a party has while `seeExhibit` runs is filed under that exhibit through `seeingEx`); `guestsNight` moves them to `last`, and `exThoughts(e, n)` lists them for the exhibit's Guests tab. Also learning (`p.edu`, `learn`, `hasSign`): info signs, field guides and the Education Center teach guests, which lifts mood, cuts litter and vandalism, brings donations, and feeds the Education rating part (`EDU`).
  Where a party stands to watch (`standNear`, tuned by `SIGHT`): a viewing platform or gallery first (they walk up onto the deck, or into the gallery, where `drawParties` hides them under the roof), else a spot along the fence. Acrylic sides weigh `glassWeight` times more and draw from `glassReach`, so a run of acrylic beside a path works as a viewing gallery; passing parties also stop for an exhibit with acrylic `glassGlance` times more often (`hasGlass`). A party on a deck (`p.deckB`) is drawn lifted to deck height, up the steps (`deckLift` in map.js). Nobody stops inside or right against a path prop (`inProp`, checked by `standOk` and by `stepOff` while standing about); they only walk past them.
- Power in view (power.js, see docs/animals.md): guest nodes carry `n.eyesore` and `n.green` from `indexGrid`, and `gridMood` in `guestsTick` turns them into mood (eyesores hurt everyone, conservationists most; green stations cheer only conservationists). Guest buildings with no power serve nothing (`servesOf`).
- Weather (guests.js, `GUEST_WX` in data.js): it never keeps guests away (demand ignores it); it costs mood out in it. `guestWeather()` once a tick gives the heat (over `hotFrom`) and cold (under `coldBelow`) mood a minute from `wxNow().temp`, the event's (`GUEST_WX.ev`, as it falls now), a thirst multiplier for the heat, and the ids of `indoor` buildings; `weatherMood(p, wg)` charges a party, nothing while it is in line at or inside an indoor building (`p.in`) or watching a vivarium (`p.watch`), times `pm(p, "weather")` (families mind more). `buy` counts jackets and every `GUEST_GEAR` item per party (`p.umbrella`, `p.jacket`, `p.beanie`, `p.trexhat`, `p.sabhat`, `p.tribag`, `p.dimebag`; they show on the sprite, see docs/art.md): carried by the whole party umbrellas cut rain and storms to `GUEST_WX.umbrella`, and jackets and beanies cut cold, snow and wind to `GUEST_WX.jacket`/`beanie` (the two multiply). Drinks marked `warm` in `MENU` (coffee, hot chocolate) set `p.warmTil`, cutting cold, snow and wind to `GUEST_WX.warm`; items marked `cool` (water, soda, slushies, ice cream) set `p.coolTil`, cutting heat to `GUEST_WX.cool`; both last `GUEST_WX.drinkMin` and are in `shiftStamps`. Umbrellas sell `WEATHER.umbrella` times as well on a wet day (`wxWet`), jackets and beanies `WEATHER.jacket` in cold, snow or wind (`wxWrapUp`, services.js). At a food stand, `wxDrink()` says which drinks the weather calls for: they score `WEATHER.drink` times higher and guests get one from `GUEST_WX.drinkThirst` thirst instead of 25. `MENU` `text` is the hover tooltip in the menu panel. Thoughts `wxHot`, `wxCold`, `wxRain`, `wxSnow`, `wxStorm`, `wxSand`, `wxWind` (`WX_THOUGHT`).
- `js/personas.js`: guest personalities, loaded right after guests.js. Every party has `p.pers` (primary, secondary; `PERSONALITIES` and `PERS` in data.js, `rollPers`). The primary counts in full and the secondary `PERS.second` as much. `pm(p, key)` blends a personality's `fx` multipliers (cached per party in `p.fxc`; neutral is 1; `pneed` for need rates) and is read all over: `serveAt` (food, shop, museum, toilet, price), `learn`, donations, queue patience, litter, mood hurt (`infra`, `walk`, `decor`), fleeing. Exhibit tastes steer `pickSight` (`tasteOf`, `centerTaste`, off `exhibitTraits(e)`: danger, kids, active, hunting, quality, rare, platform) and reactions run from `seeExhibit` (`personaSee`, `PERS_SEE`) and on the way out (`personaLeaves`: conservationists sniff out a park that is all show, paleo-nerds judge the collection). Hooligans (`p.rowdy` is true for primary or secondary) may sneak in without a ticket (`sneaksIn`, `p.sneaked`, checked in `guestsArrive`) and tease animals at fences (`teaseTick`). Calling security from a guest's panel: `ejectGuest` (`ejectProblem`, `ejectTick`, `escortOut`, `partyStatus`). Ordinary parties stop (`p.hold`) until a guard comes within `PERS.escortRadius`, then are walked to the gate unhappy; primary hooligans keep moving and the guard has to catch them (`guardWatch` in security.js). There are no rides, playgrounds or petting zoos in the game yet, so those likes aren't wired. To add a personality, add an entry to `PERSONALITIES`, a `PERS_TASTE`/`PERS_SEE` function if it reacts to exhibits, and `THOUGHTS` for what it says.
  Guests walk and live at `GUEST.pace` of staff speed (`spd` includes it; `guestsTick` runs needs, mood drain, vandalism and teasing on `gdt = dt * pace`; `stay` is divided by it), so one can be clicked before it's gone while a trip still feels the same per meter. Parties are dots in their primary personality's color in `drawParties` (the mood-color toggle stays); a tap in select mode finds one by screen distance (`guestAt`) and opens `sel = {kind:"guest"}` (`selItem` reads `parties`), drawn by `guestHtml`/`guestLive` in guestui.js, which updates in place from the frame loop instead of redrawing the panel.
  Guests have no curfew: `until` is arrival plus the stay (`newParty`), nothing caps it at closing, so a late arrival stays on overnight and `shiftStamps` moves `until` back a day across the 6 AM rollover. A party heads for the gate (`goHome`) when `until` is near, its mood drops below `quitBelow`, it has an accident, a loose dangerous animal scares it, security escorts it out (`escortOut`) or the park is closed. Arrivals follow `arrivalShare` plus the night trickle.
- `js/services.js`: guest services. Menus on food and gift shells (`b.menu`, `MENU`, `servesOf`, `serveAt`, `willPay`), benches and picnic areas, trash bins and litter (`state.litter` grid, `trashCheck`), restroom dirt, and the night cleaning crew (`servicesNight`, `cleaningBill`). Trash thrown into exhibits: `throwTick` (from the guest loop) lets rowdy parties, and others holding trash, throw it over an open exhibit's fence (`THROWN` in data.js, `throwChance`; cut by working bins, guards via `guardNear`, netting, and a `nofeed` sign within `THROWN.signReach`, `hasNoFeed`), adding to `e.trash` (what animals do with it: medicine.js).
  Museum attractions (end of services.js): `MUSEUM_EVENTS` in data.js (Great Dying, K-Pg Impact, Hadean Earth, Great Oxygenation, Cambrian Explosion, Tetrapods) are recorded by the Time Travel Lab expeditions to their event sites (`TIME_SITES` with an `event`: Chengjiang, Miguasha, the Siberian Traps and Chicxulub on the map, Hadean Earth and the Great Oxygenation under Deep Time). They reuse the genome machinery (`sc.dna[id]`, `genomeInfo(id).event`; `placeOf` gives the event's trip), gated by the `education` tech. A finished record is on offer in an Education Center's dropdown (`b.shows`, `showsReady`, `addShow`/`dropShow`, `MUSEUM.slots`); `showsLearn`/`showsJoy` add to a visit in `serveAt`, `showsAppeal` to `planParty`, `showsUpkeep` to the daily bill, at full strength (a museum record has to be complete). UI: `museumHtml` in guestui.js.
  Education Center focus and modules (also end of services.js): `b.focus` (`EDU_FOCUS`: conservation donations, spectacle appeal, family mood and learning, research points) works at `focusPower(b)`, 1 plus the `boost` of each installed module (`b.mods`, `EDU_MODULES`: theater, fossils, auditorium, each gated by a Research Lab tech `ed*`, `EDU_CENTER.moduleSlots`). `eduLearn`/`eduJoy`/`eduAppeal`/`eduUpkeep` sum museum attractions, modules and focus, and are what `serveAt`, `planParty` and the daily bill use; `slotsOf` adds the auditorium's seats in the guests.js queue. UI: `focusHtml`/`modulesHtml` in guestui.js. Live modules (`live` in `EDU_MODULES`: labwindow, nursery, botanical, simroom, diorama, touch) scale their effects and focus boost by `modLive(id)`: how many science departments have work going (lab), or clones growing plus a hatching today (`sc.hatchDay`, set when a clone finishes) for the nursery, or finished plant DNA plus growing beds for the botanical hall, or finished Time Travel Lab event records for the simulation room, or biomes with animals in them for the diorama, or finished animal genomes for the touch gallery; `dept` lists the science buildings needed to install one. All nine planned modules are in; to add one, add an entry to `EDU_MODULES` plus a tech.
- `js/custodians.js`: custodians (`ccrew`, `state.staff.custodians`) who restock food stands, gift shops and hotels with guest goods (`pickHaul` with `GUEST_GOODS`), scrub restrooms, empty bins, and sweep litter, working from a Custodial Closet.
  - The evening restock: stands, shops and hotels (`restocked(b)`, stores with `vendor`) hold shelves plus a back room. From 7 PM (`restockEvening`, when the truck comes) to the end of the day, `pickHaul` gives them its top class (0) and fills them to 90% of `storeTarget`, ahead of every chore (`custDecide`). Through the day they're only restocked under `LOGI.lowMark` of their target (`lowStock`), which also draws a red "!" over them on the map (`.lowmark`, map.js `render`). At the rollover, before hotel guests use toiletries and anything spoils, `restockCheck` (logistics.js, called from `endDay`) sets `b.missed` on any left under `LOGI.restockedAt` and toasts the goods storage ran out of (`inStorage`) apart from the ones nobody got to in time. The panels (`restockRows`, guestui.js) show "Running low.", "Not restocked last night." and Call custodian.
  - Call custodian: a `cust` call in `CALLS` (keepers.js) whose `ex` is a building. `callProblem` refuses it when the building is full (`shortGoods`) or storage has none of what it's short of. The called custodian drops a chore or a haul not yet picked up at the next node (`takeCustCall`, in `custodiansTick`), then fills it a load at a time (`pickHaul(..., only)`), and the call ends when it's at its target or storage is out.
- `js/security.js`: vandalism by rowdy, unhappy guests, with camera posts (`camera` path prop, `SECURITY.postRadius`) and office cameras feeding `onCamera` (`vandalTick`, `vandalize`, `deterrence`, broken props via `propCond`/`isBroken`, `b.graffiti`) and security guards (`gcrew`, `state.staff.guards`) who patrol, deter, catch vandals, and answer cameras from a Security Office. Lamp light (end of security.js, `LAMP` in data.js): `lampSpots`/`lightAt(x, y)` give a working lamp post's pool (leaning toward `propFront`), `darkLevel()` is the night wash 0 to 1, `indexLight(nodes)` (from `buildGuestGraph`, next to `indexDecor`) marks each path node `n.lit`, counts `darkStretches` (Guests tab) and fills `litExhibits`. At night an unlit stretch drains guest mood (`LAMP.fear` times `pm(p, "dark")`, the "too dark" thought), `pickSight` weights unlit exhibits down (`darkPull`), lit spots cut `deterrence` and unlit ones raise it, and `onCamera` only sees an unlit spot some of the time. `drawLights` (map.js) cuts stepped holes (`LAMP.steps`, `LAMP.visRadius`) in `#nightWash`, now an SVG with a mask (`#washMask`, `#washHoles`), so lit ground shows at day color; each step is one group at one opacity, so overlapping pools never add up. `#lightSvg` (screen-blended, above it) only holds a faint warm tint (`LAMP.tint`) and a small glow hanging just under each lamp head (`LAMP.headH`, `LAMP.headR`). By day lamps work as before.
- `js/lodging.js`: hotels (`lodge`, `resort`, `isHotel`, `LODGING`). Guests book rooms overnight (`lodgingNight`, `state.lodging.stays`), and next morning they start at the hotel with no ticket (`hotelGuestsArrive`). Rooms use toiletries (merch in `b.store`, which custodians restock) and get dirty (`b.dirt`, which custodians clean). Unlocking is checked in `hotelLocked`.
- `js/staff.js`: every walking worker in one place, loaded after security.js. `STAFF_TYPES` (keeper, aquarist, vet, mechanic, custodian, guard: `list`, `crew`, `hire`, `status`, `sync`, `ok`), `staffRoster`/`staffById`/`staffCrew`, `hireStaff`/`fireStaff` (fire hands back carried food, moves and hunts), `energyOf` (`s.stamina`; keepers and aquarists (keeper records with `role:"aqua"`) tire walking, mucking and carrying in keepers.js, the rest lose `STAFF.workDrain`/`idleDrain` a minute in `staffTick`, everyone resets in `staffNight`), and morale (`s.morale`, drifts toward `moraleTarget` from `moraleFactors`, which are also the thoughts; `STAFF` in data.js). Hiring and firing happen ONLY in the park office's Staff tab (`staffTabHtml` in staffui.js); department panels just show `staffNote`. Scientists are counts, not people (`state.science.crew`), so they get hire/fire rows in the same tab but no panel.
- `js/staffui.js`: the Staff tab (also holds the Work zones section; the Draw Work Zone build tool is in the Staff Buildings group), the tapped-worker panel (`sel = {kind:"staff"}`, `staffHtml`/`staffLive`, found on the map by `staffAt`, ringed by `drawStaffRing`), and the Fire "Are you sure?" dialog (`staffFireDialog`, via `askConfirm`).
- Viewing Galleries (`BUILDINGS.gallery`, `GALLERY`, dev mode only): a building against a pool's fence (`isPool`), outside it, `GALLERY.gap` off the fence with its door (`face`) away from the water (`findGallerySpot`, `galleryHover`, `galleryTap` in map.js; drawn by `building34` from `STAND34.gallery`, a glass front). It carries `exhibitId` like a platform, so it rides with and goes with its exhibit, but it stands on the park's own ground: `parkGround(b)` is true for it (and for every building without `exhibitId`) and false for platforms, and fence pieces, pens, exhibit moves and reshapes check it. `viewFactor` (escapes.js) takes the better of the platforms' and the galleries' boost (`GALLERY.view`, `more` a gallery after the first, up to `most`). The exhibit panel's Guests tab has a Viewing galleries row for pools.
