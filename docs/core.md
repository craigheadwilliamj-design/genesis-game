# Core: data, sim loop, science, saved state

Moved out of CLAUDE.md. Update this file (not CLAUDE.md) when the code it describes changes.

- `js/data.js`: all tuning constants and content (`SPECIES`, `BUILDINGS`, `BARRIERS`, `TECH`, `GOALS`, `KEEPER`, `MAINT`, `POWER`, `ESCAPE`, `HEALTH`, `MEDICINE`, `VET`, ...). Balance changes go here.
- Calendar: `state.day` is the counter everything uses; `CALENDAR` (data.js) and `dateOf`/`fmtDate(day)` (sim.js) turn it into "3 Mar Y2" for every player-facing date (HUD, history, toasts, game over). Nothing is saved for it.
- `js/spritepack.js` (generated by `tools/packsprites.py`, loads first): `SPRITE_PACK` and `spriteUrl(path)`; see Sprite pack.
- `js/geometry.js`: pure shape math (`area`, `perimeter`, `inPoly`, `segProj`, `shapesOverlap`, ...).
- `js/sim.js`: the core loop `tick(dtMin)` → `endDay()`, plus guest demand, money (`spend`/`earn`/`canAfford`), happiness (`exhibitReport`), rating, species pull on guests (`speciesDraw`: appeal stretched by `GUEST.drawPower` around `GUEST.drawPivot`, so showstoppers dominate and low-appeal animals draw little), goals, and save migration (`newPark`, `upgradeSave`; one-time conversions are gated by a flag on the save that `newPark` sets, like `vivDish`, `vivLay` and `vivScene`). The park's own biome is `state.biome` (`parkBiome()`, picked on the start screen's New Park form (`#dlgStart`, main.js `biomeCards`/`pickBiome`/`showStart`) and locked for the park's life: the park office only shows it, and old saves keep theirs): the ground is drawn in `BIOMES[x].park` with the `#p-<biome>` pattern, and new exhibits start on it. Also land for sale (`PARCELS` in data.js, `state.parcels`, `ownsParcel`, `parcelPrice`, `buyParcel`, `ownedRects`/`ownedEdges`/`inOwned`; the Buy Land tool `parcels` and `insidePlot` are in map.js, and `state.boundary` is just the home plot now). The entrance (`state.gate`) is on the park's south edge, so land only grows east, west and north: `PARCELS.ys` ends at the entrance line (`SOUTH_LINE` in sim.js), nothing south is listed, drawn as for sale (`SALE_CELLS` is what the overlay, tap and menu use) or buyable (`parcelSouth`, `parcelProblem`). The south edge is a hard wall drawn in `render` (map.js, open at the gate), and since nothing south is ever owned, `insidePlot` rejects every build, path, fence, water and decor past it; `clampView` stops the camera scrolling far past the land. There is no plot limit: each plot already bought adds `PARCELS.growth` (12%) of the base price to the next one (`parcelPrice`; `parcelsBought` is shown in the Buy Land bar). `LEGACY_SOUTH_YS` (data.js) are cell rows south of the line that exist only so old saves that bought them keep that land (`PARCEL_CELLS` includes them, `SALE_CELLS` doesn't); `upgradeSave` never removes owned parcels. Also shared helpers: `uid`, `dept`/`hasDept` (is a science building up), `money`, `fmtArea`, `fmtClock`, `SPECIES_BY_ID`.
- Dev-mode: `devMode()` (sim.js) reads localStorage `genesis-dev` (off by default, never in `state`); click the version label (`#verLabel`, `GAME_VERSION` in ui.js) at the foot of the Main Menu 5 times in a row to toggle it (main.js; the build menu redraws at once). The toggle also sets `devFlag`, which `devMode()` reads first, so it holds for the visit even where storage is blocked, and it shows the pixel editor's `#pixelBtn` at once (the rest of dev mode still wants a reload).
- `js/science.js`: ORACLE/GHOST/TAR/CERES logic, loaded right after sim.js. Every scientist is one bay holding one job or none (`BAY_JOBS`, `bayCount`, `freeBay`, `fireScientist`; each job carries its `bay`), so hiring is the only way the work lists grow; GHOST's bays are its drones (`bayCount("temporal")` is `sc.drones`), bought, not hired; `baysHtml` in scienceui.js draws them in a fixed order. Everything is timed in park minutes (`nowMin()`, jobs carry `start`/`end`) and finishes mid-day in `scienceTick(dtMin)`, not at the rollover. `tick(dtMin)` (sim.js) cuts a step at the daily rollover (the rest carries into the next day) and at `scienceNext()`, the next job end, so a job completes the minute its `end` passes whatever the step size; `tickStep` is the single step. ORACLE projects (`startProject`, kinds `tech`/`refine`, one per researcher; a researcher with no project is on general research and earns `RESEARCH_PER_PALEO` points a day, `stopProject` puts one back, and guests add `RESEARCH_GUEST` points times what they learned as they leave in guests.js; a project remembers its `cost` for refunds), GHOST (below), TAR clones (`orderClone`, upgrades `fast1/2`), and CERES growing beds (`growBatch`: landscape plants by period and size into `ceres.pots` (keys like `Jurassic-large`), medicine batches; plant DNA is one genome per Mesozoic/Paleozoic period, `PLANT_DNA[period]`, which GHOST collects once ORACLE has researched the era's flora). CERES's daily Paleoflora food output is NOT here: it's in keepers.js.
- GHOST (science.js; design in `DISCOVERY_PLAN.md`): a timeline of real sites in deep time, flown by drones. `TIMELINE` (data.js) gives every period an equal bar split equally into epochs; `EPOCHS`, `yearsAt`/`uAt` (slider position to years ago, log scale inside an epoch, floor `TIMELINE_FLOOR`), `fmtYearsAgo`. Epochs are display only: `period` stays the unit of every mechanic. `TIMELINE_MAPS` picks an epoch's or period's map (`sprites/timeline/<name>.png`, 260 x 130 Mollweide, drafted by `tools/timeline/genmaps.py` from the PALEOMAP PaleoDEMs, Scotese & Wright 2018, CC BY 4.0: keep the credit on the GHOST panel). `TIME_SITES` (data.js, written by `tools/timeline/placesites.py` from `tools/timeline/sites.py`; never edit the block by hand) are real localities with a `from`/`to` window in years, a marker pixel per map (`at`, placed by plate reconstruction and nudged so markers never touch) and `fauna` species ids, or an `event` (a `MUSEUM_EVENTS` id; the two older than the slider have no `at` and show under Deep Time). A site shows for any epoch its window touches (`sitesIn`), only with animals `speciesAvailable` shows (`siteFauna`, `siteShown`); it belongs to the period it starts in (`sitePeriod`), whose `TIME_PERIODS` entry sets its trip. Scouting (`sendDrone("scout", site)`, `scoutProblem`, `scoutCost`): the first trip lights the site (`sc.seen[id]`, `isScouted`); each unseen animal turns up with `scoutChance` (`SCOUT.find` by size), at most `SCOUT.max` a trip, and one turning up is discovered (`sc.unlocked`). `siteSeen`, `siteComplete`. Expeditions (`sendDrone("dig", site, id)`, `expeditionProblem`, `tripOdds(id, site)`): to a lit site (`siteLit`) for one of `siteTargets` (the animals seen there, the period's `PLANT_DNA` once ORACLE has the flora, or the event); a genome takes 2 to 8 finds by size, bonus traces go to another animal seen at the same site, and an animal can't be targeted until the park reaches its stars (`starsNeed`; scouting never is; TAR no longer checks stars). Drones (`sc.drones`, `DRONES`: 1 to 5, the first comes with GHOST, `dronePrice`, `buyDrone`; not staff, no wage) fly one trip each (`sc.trips`: `{kind, site, sp, bay (the drone), cost, start, end}`); all share a mark (`GHOST_MK`, `droneMk`, `droneCut`: Mk. I to V, ORACLE techs `ghostmk2`-`ghostmk5`) that cuts empty-handed trips by a share. `tripReturns` files a post-mission report (`sc.reports`, `[text, headline?]` lines like "Tiktaalik Discovered", "Site Complete", "Tiktaalik Sample Found", "Came back empty handed"; `dismissReport`). `placeOf`/`placeName` take a site (or, in old saves, a period or event).
- Genome completeness is the only genome stat (`sc.dna[id] = {genome}`; there is no separate quality, and `upgradeSave` drops old `quality` fields and refunds the removed GHOST quality upgrades `ghostq1/2`). TAR clones once a genome is `CLONE_GENOME.min` (60) % complete (`cloneOpen`), and the clone's DNA quality `a.q` (`cloneQuality`, `CLONE_GENOME.q0` at the minimum up to 100 when whole) sets how healthy it is (sickly under 70, frail under 50, see `HEALTH`). Genome therapy lifts a clone toward `cloneQuality` of the lab's current genome. A partial genome can also fail in the incubator (`cloneFailChance`: `CLONE_GENOME.failMax` at the minimum, 0 at 100%, never in sandbox): `orderClone` rolls it and gives the job `fail` (the minute it dies) and `why`; `scienceTick` calls `failClone`, which sets `failed`/`stop` (progress freezes there, `cloneProgress`), keeps the bay and the fee spent, and the job sits until `discardClone` (the Discard button; `scienceNext` and the completion filter skip failed jobs). `cloneCard` (scienceui.js) is the progress card in the TAR panel and the exhibit's Cloning tab, with a red CLONE FAILED overlay when failed; `cloneLive` (main.js frame loop) moves the bars without rebuilding the panel.

## Key globals
- `state.science`: `points`, `crew` (paleo, gene, botanist), `tech` (TECH ids, plus `ref-<Period>`), `projects`, `unlocked` (discovered animal ids), `dna` (animal, plant and event genomes, `PLANT_DNA`), `drones`, `seen` (site id -> animals scouted there), `trips` (drones out), `reports`, `clones`. Saves from before the GHOST rework (no `discoV`) get a drone per Temporal Researcher, their unlocked animals' sites scouted, ORACLE species projects refunded, and trips out turned into expeditions to a site (`upgradeSave`). `state.ceres`: Paleoflora `stock`, `meds`, landscape plants `pots` (keyed `<period>-<size>`), `beds`, `auto` (keyed by era for medicine, plus `food`). Paleoflora food also grows passively (`PALEOFLORA`); a `food` bed (`CERES_GROW.food`) adds `amount` to the stock, and `auto.food` keeps one running while there's room under `ceresCap()`. `meds` holds up to `medCap()` doses of each era's medicine separately; an era set in `auto` gets a batch whenever its own stock plus what's growing leaves room for one, and the era lowest across CERES and the PMC goes first.
- `state`: the whole saved game (JSON-serialized). **Anything that must survive a reload lives in `state`.**
- `derived`: computed from `state` by `recompute()` (reach, reports, demand, rating parts). Call `recompute()` after layout changes.
- `state.zones` / `state.logi`: work zones and logistics bookkeeping. Food is physical: it lives in `b.store` of stations, docks, warehouses, cold stores, and farms, and the PMC holds medicine. Exhibits only get what keepers carry. Stands and shops sell from their own `b.store` of guest goods (snacks, drinks, merch) once `state.logi.guestFrom` has passed; custodians carry it to them.
- `kGraph`: the staff walking graph. Rebuild it with `buildKeeperGraph()` after paths, buildings or gates change.
- `crew` / `mcrew` / `vcrew` / `ccrew` / `gcrew`: live keeper, mechanic, vet, custodian, and guard walkers. These are NOT saved, so mirror anything persistent onto `state.staff.*` (see `setCarry`, `transfer.cargo`).
- `parties` / `gGraph`: live guest parties and their footpath graph (rebuild with `buildGuestGraph()` alongside `buildKeeperGraph()`). Parties are NOT saved. Guests pay where they're served, and their mood when they leave goes into `state.today.moodSum`/`moodN` and `state.guestLog`, which set guest comfort and word of mouth.
- Building facing: `b.face` (0-3 = north, east, south, west) is saved on every non-prop building and says which wall has the door, sign and windows (`faceEdge(P, face)` and `doorPoint(b)` in geometry.js). `upgradeSave` gives an older building the side whose wall middle is nearest a path chunk (south when there are no chunks). `buildGuestGraph` puts a building's guest stop at the path point nearest its door point, falling back to the point nearest its middle when none is in range. `recompute` reach tries the door side first (`doorNear`, pathcells.js) and then any side, so a door facing away from the path still works; the placing ghost only says "Its door faces away from the path." Staff stops (`buildKeeperGraph`) still attach at the middle. Path props have `rot` instead.
- `events`: hooks the sim calls (`toast`, `changed`, `dayEnded`, `gameOver`), wired in main.js.
- The park day runs on a clock from 6 AM to 11 PM and then jumps back to 6 AM. The sim counts in PARK minutes: `DAY_MIN` (720) of them make one day and `MINUTES_PER_SECOND` a second on screen (a day is 240 s), so per-minute and per-day rates are unchanged. The clock is only how a park minute is shown: `CLOCK_SCALE` (`DAY_CLOCK` 1020 / `DAY_MIN`) clock minutes each. `state.minute` is park minutes since the daily rollover, which happens at `ROLLOVER_HOUR` (6 AM): `endDay()` and every `*Night()` run then (bills, `rollWeather`, `staffNight`, `lodgingNight`), and `state.minute` goes back to 0. `clockMin(m)` (sim.js) gives the clock minute of the day for a `state.minute`, `minuteAtClock(c)` goes back (only for clock times from 6 AM to 11 PM), and `fmtClock` takes a clock minute (the HUD shows `fmtClock(clockMin())`). Science stamps are continuous park minutes (`nowMin() = (day - 1) * DAY_MIN + minute`), shown as clock time by `whenText`/`spanText`. Daylight is `DAWN_CLOCK`/`DUSK_CLOCK` (5 AM, 9 PM, so the whole day is light until 9 PM and dark 9 to 11; `DAWN_MIN`/`DUSK_MIN` as `state.minute` values, `isNight` in behavior.js): `awake`/`napLull` read the clock hour (nocturnal animals are up all night), and `nightDark()` + `NIGHT` (data.js) drive the `#nightWash` map overlay (one `opacity` change per HUD update, only when the step changes). Guests: the day's crowd arrives in a hump from `GUEST.arriveFrom` to `arriveTo` (`arrivalShare`), plus a thin round-the-clock trickle (`GUEST.trickle` a day at 5 stars, `trickleLow` at 0, `trickleRate()`; it is carved out of `derived.demand`, never on top of it). Nobody is flushed at closing: a party leaves when its stay (`until`) is up, so guests are still in the park after dark and across the rollover, and the park is never fully empty. Guest timers are `state.minute` stamps (`until`, `cool`, `linger`, `readyAt`, ...), so `shiftStamps(DAY_MIN)` (guests.js, called by `endDay`) moves them back a day at the rollover; any new guest timestamp must be added there. Closing: `state.parkClosed` (saved, default false; the Gate section of the Park Office's Park tab, action `toggleClosed`): `guestsArrive` and hotel guests do nothing, every party still inside gets `goHome`, the HUD shows `#closedTag`; wages and upkeep continue. Old saves (no `clockV`) are shifted by the old opening time in `upgradeSave`, so science stamps stay exact.
