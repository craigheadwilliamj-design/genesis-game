# UI, map rendering, menus

Moved out of CLAUDE.md. Update this file (not CLAUDE.md) when the code it describes changes.

- `js/menu.js`: the build sidebar (`BUILD_MENU`, `buildMenu`, `refreshMenu`; Park Plants and Statues dropdowns, statue locks via `data-lock`). The Park Plants and Quaternary plants dropdowns list the same 51 plants (the named park plants and the Quaternary biome plants), by design: `parkPlantKeys`, `plantRow`. Both group by biome (`BIOMES` order, a `snote` heading each) and order each biome small, medium, large (`bySize`), with the size on the row. Groups and dropdowns are generated from the data, so prices follow `BUILDINGS`, `BARRIERS` and `fenceRate`. Loads before map.js.
- `js/parkmap.js`: the Park Map dialog (`#dlgParkMap`, HUD `#mapBtn`, key M; Esc, Close or a click outside closes it). `drawParkMap(canvas)` paints the whole park top-down in pixel blocks straight from `state` (owned land in the park biome color, unowned dimmed, water, exhibits in biome color with barrier-colored fence outlines, `fenceGrid` pieces, paths, buildings as `BUILDINGS[type].color` blocks with science ones pale, the south wall and entrance gap, guest and staff dots, and a white box for the main view's viewport). It never touches the main renderer or `tilt`. Drawn on open and every `PMAP.refreshMs`; a click on the map recenters the main camera and closes it. Loads just before main.js.
- `js/map.js`: SVG rendering, camera (the 3/4 view is the only main view: `tilt` is a constant `true`, there is no toggle or stored setting, and the old flat top-down `!tilt` branches are dead code left in place to remove piece by piece), build tools (including the Move tool: `moveTarget`, `moveBy`, `moveProblem`, `dropMove`; and `fenceSel`, the fence the fence tool lays. The fence tool is `tool === "exhibit"` but not a draw: a left drag lays grid fence (`fdrag`, `fenceBar`, `fenceDragStat`, `endFenceDrag`, preview in `renderOverlay`), and in Bulldoze a drag or tap on a fence lifts pieces (`pieceNear`, `fhover`). The middle or right mouse button always pans), and drawing guests (`drawParties`). Everything placed or moved snaps to the 1 m grid (`GRID_STEP`, `snapAt`, `landPoint`); there's no free placement and no toggle. Placement checks `exhibitProblem`/`pathProblem`, `removeItem` (bulldoze), `findItem`, `insidePlot`. Also defines `$`, `esc` and `nodeKey`, which sim files use too. Its `moveProblem(kind, it)` is for the Move tool; moving animals between exhibits uses keepers.js's `keeperMoveProblem(e)`.
- `js/ui.js`: HUD, side panel (`ui.panel()`), toasts, catalog and dialogs, and panel click actions. The Park Office is the side panel with nothing selected (`overviewHtml`, tab in `officeTab`, `officeTabs`): Park (name input `#officeName`, rating, history, Main Menu button), Money (ledger, supplies, ticket price), Science, Guests, Staff, Grants. The HUD park name (`#parkName`) and the Park button both call `openParkOffice(tab)` in main.js. The Main Menu (`officeTab = "menu"`, `mainMenuHtml`) holds sandbox mode, How to play and Restart (`askConfirm`, then `showStart()`). There is no `#dlgMenu` dialog any more. The exhibit panel's tabs (`exTabs`): Exhibit, Landscape Needs (`landHtml`: plants, rocks, water), Habitat Props (`propsHtml`: the barns in `HABITAT_PROPS` from menu.js, plus shelter cover), Behavior (`behaviorHtml`), Cloning, Health.
- `js/scienceui.js`: the ORACLE (Park management / Animals / Paleo-Flora tabs), GHOST (period tabs with genome bars), TAR and CERES panels and their clicks.
- `js/logiui.js`: panels for stores, the dock, zones (the park office Staff tab's Work zones section, `zonesSectionHtml`: zone list, Draw a zone, supply lines toggle), and the park-office supply summary, plus their click/change handlers. Also defines `goodName`, which custodians.js uses.
- `js/guestui.js`: the park office's Guests section and the panels for food and gift shells (menu picker and prices), restrooms, bins, and seats.
- `js/main.js`: the rAF loop (`frame` → `step`), saving (localStorage plus the claude.ai `db` capability), and `startWith`.

Guests are drawn in `guestLayer`, above the whole world, so nothing in the world can cover them by depth. Two things stand in for that: `hidden34` hides a guest standing behind a building or vivarium tank (`occ34`), and `wallCut34` clips a guest standing behind a solid far-side exhibit fence (`wall34`, brick, stone, concrete, hedge and the like) so only what's above the fence's top edge shows. See-through fences and open canopies don't cover guests.
