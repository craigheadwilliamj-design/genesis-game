# UI, map rendering, menus

Moved out of CLAUDE.md. Update this file (not CLAUDE.md) when the code it describes changes.

- `js/menu.js`: the left tool column (`buildMenu`): small Bulldoze and Move buttons (`data-tool`; tapping the active one again goes back to Select, as does Esc), then four big category buttons (`BUILD_CATS`: Buildings, Landscaping, Exhibits, Paths). Each opens its tray along the bottom of the screen (`.tray` in `#sideBody`, `sideOpen(cat)` in map.js, open one in `sideCat`). The tray's bottom bar is menu A (`.trayA`): Buildings lists its groups as `GROUP_ICONS` buttons (each a `.sitem.sgroup`, its tools flattened by `flatRows` so Dining, Retail and Hotels spread out, and Logistics also holds the Supply lines toggle). The other trays list their group's `menuRow`s. A plain tool in menu A picks it at once. A row that holds a list (`.srow.head`) opens that list (`.ssub`) as menu B, a bar above (one at a time). Plant lists keep their biome filter and biome labels, and Rock and Boulder show their shape picker as a bar above while that tool is on (`.mapwrap.tool-land-rock`). Item notes become button tooltips. A building with `BLD_PICS` shows its front picture as its button picture. The trays stay open while you build. Esc drops the tool first, then closes the tray. One delegated click handler on `#sideMenu` (map.js) runs it all, so `buildMenu()` can redraw it any time, and a mouse wheel scrolls a bar sideways. Also `BUILD_MENU`, `refreshMenu`; Park Plants and Statues dropdowns, statue locks via `data-lock`). The Park Plants and Quaternary plants dropdowns list the same 51 plants (the named park plants and the Quaternary biome plants), by design: `parkPlantKeys`, `plantRow`. Both group by biome (`BIOMES` order, a `snote` heading each) and order each biome small, medium, large (`bySize`), with the size on the row. Groups and dropdowns are generated from the data, so prices follow `BUILDINGS`, `BARRIERS` and `fenceRate`. Loads before map.js.
- `js/parkmap.js`: the Park Map dialog (`#dlgParkMap`, right-column `#mapBtn`, key M; Esc, Close or a click outside closes it). `drawParkMap(canvas)` paints the whole park top-down in pixel blocks straight from `state` (owned land in the park biome color, unowned dimmed, water, exhibits in biome color with barrier-colored fence outlines, `fenceGrid` pieces, paths, buildings as `BUILDINGS[type].color` blocks with science ones pale, the south wall and entrance gap, guest and staff dots, and a white box for the main view's viewport). It never touches the main renderer or `tilt`. Drawn on open and every `PMAP.refreshMs`; a click on the map recenters the main camera and closes it. Loads just before main.js.
- `js/map.js`: SVG rendering, camera (the 3/4 view is the only main view: `tilt` is a constant `true`, there is no toggle or stored setting, and the old flat top-down `!tilt` branches are dead code left in place to remove piece by piece), build tools (including the Move tool: `moveTarget`, `moveBy`, `moveProblem`, `dropMove`; land items (rocks, plants, shelters, statues) move too, via `landMoveSpot` and `dropLand`, see landscape.md; and `fenceSel`, the fence the fence tool lays. The fence tool is `tool === "exhibit"` but not a draw: a left drag lays grid fence (`fdrag`, `fenceBar`, `fenceDragStat`, `endFenceDrag`, preview in `renderOverlay`), and in Bulldoze a drag or tap on a fence lifts pieces (`pieceNear`, `fhover`). The middle or right mouse button always pans), and drawing guests (`drawParties`). Everything placed or moved snaps to the 1 m grid (`GRID_STEP`, `snapAt`, `landPoint`); there's no free placement and no toggle. Placing a building (`placeGhost`): everything that isn't an `onPath` prop goes on the 1 m grid at the pointer, square to the map, and Rotate (`rotateTool`, `rotateMoved`, R) turns it a quarter turn; everything, path props included, faces north, south, east or west (`rot` stays in 45° units and moves 2 at a time). The turn also swings the door: a new building faces south (`face` 2), each quarter turn adds one (`ghost.face = (2 + rot / 2) % 4`), `placeBuilding` saves it as `b.face` and `rotateMoved` bumps it; the ghost and the Move selection show a door arrow out of that wall (`doorArrowSvg`). Its door wall then slides onto the nearest grid line (`doorOnGrid`, up to half a meter; also after `rotateMoved` turns it; a picture card facing north snaps its base line instead), so a building of any depth, like the 2.6 m food cart, stands flush against a path, whose edges are on whole meters. It never snaps to a path or takes a path's angle. It is refused over path chunks (`bldOnPath` in pathcells.js: `shapeOnChunks` plus old path lines on `pathSolid`, the footprint less the back `backOver` meters of a picture card like the food cart; `pathChunkProblem` uses the same shape), exhibits, buildings, water, plants, rocks and fences (`shapeHitsLandscape`); flush against a chunk edge is fine. Not being near a path is only a warning ("No path nearby, so it won't work until one reaches it"), using the same test as `recompute`: `nearCells(shape, REACH - PATH_HALF_WIDTH, guests)` (staff reach for `dept` buildings; `serviceOnly` ones also need a staff `"s"` chunk, via `nearCells`'s `kind` argument). The Move tool's `moveProblem` uses the same overlap rules; `rotateMoved` turns in place about the middle or refuses, with no sliding to fit. `onPath` props (bench, bin, lamp, camera, donation box, signs) also sit on the 1 m grid at the pointer, but only where every footprint corner is on a guest or bridge chunk (`shapeOnGuestPath` in pathcells.js, refused with "Keep it on a path."), so they can stand anywhere on a wide path. Their front (`propFront`) defaults to the nearest exhibit fence (`EDU.signReach` for signs, 8 m otherwise) or path edge, whichever is nearer (`propFacing`, due north, south, east or west, south if neither), and Rotate adds quarter turns to that (`rot`). A prop whose footprint overlaps a fence slides away from what it faces until clear. Overlap with other buildings, props and exhibits is refused as usual, and `moveProblem` applies the same on-path rule (the Move tool and `rotateMoved` share it). `shapesOverlap` also counts a shape sitting exactly on another. Placement checks `exhibitProblem`/`pathProblem`, `removeItem` (bulldoze), `findItem`, `insidePlot`. Also defines `$`, `esc` and `nodeKey`, which sim files use too. Its `moveProblem(kind, it)` is for the Move tool; moving animals between exhibits uses keepers.js's `keeperMoveProblem(e)`.
- Bulldozing (map.js): a click removes the thing at once, on the map (`bulldozeTap`) and from a panel's `data-action="demolish"` button (`demolishRow`, path and fence panels) alike; both go through `bulldozeItem`. Only costly removals ask with `askConfirm` (ui.js): exhibits (`confirmExhibitRemoval`), gates (`confirmGateRemoval`), buildings `asksBulldoze` picks (`BUILDINGS[t].dept`, or `confirm:true` in data.js: the hotels, restaurant and Education Center), and a fence lift that opens a gap in a closed grid exhibit (`endFenceDrag` names the exhibits; a loose fence lifts at once). Hovering in Bulldoze outlines the target in red (`isDoomed`).
- `js/ui.js`: HUD, side panel (`ui.panel()`), toasts, the Paleopedia (`catalogHtml`, `ppCard`) and dialogs, and panel click actions. A vivarium's panel has Scene (`vivSceneHtml`: Era and Biome selects, which also set its biome) and Fittings and Enrichment (`vivToysHtml`). The Park Office is the side panel with nothing selected (`overviewHtml`, tab in `officeTab`, `officeTabs`): Park (name input `#officeName`, rating, history, Main Menu button), Finances (key `money`: ledger, supplies, ticket price), Science, Guests, Staff, Grants. The panel (`#aside`) floats over the map beside the right column and shows only while something is picked or the Park Office is open (`officeOpen`). Its ✕ (`#panelClose`), Esc and `closePanel()` shut it, and picking anything clears `officeOpen`. The right column (`#rtools`, wired in main.js) holds Office (`#officeBtn`, toggles `openParkOffice(tab)`), then the Research Lab, GHOST, the Genetics Lab (cloning) and the Botany Lab (paleo-botany) (`data-dept`: they select that building, or toast to build it first, and show pressed while their panel is open), then Paleopedia and Park Map. The HUD (`ui.hud`) is a row of `.hbox` frames: money, stars, guests in the park (today's count in its tooltip), weather (`#wxLabel`, placeholder `WX_ICON` pictures), date, day bar and clock, the speed buttons, and ☰ (`#menuBtn`). ☰ opens the Main Menu and pauses (`menuPaused`), and closing the panel picks the speed back up. H hides both columns and the panel (focus mode). These are greybox placeholders: `.hbox`, `.tbtn` and the zoom buttons are the hooks for their pixel art. The Main Menu (`officeTab = "menu"`, `mainMenuHtml`) holds sandbox mode, How to play and Restart (`askConfirm`, then `showStart()`). There is no `#dlgMenu` dialog any more. The exhibit panel's tabs (`exTabs`): Exhibit, Landscape Needs (`landHtml`: plants, rocks, water), Habitat Props (`propsHtml`: the barns in `HABITAT_PROPS` from menu.js, plus shelter cover), Behavior (`behaviorHtml`), Cloning, Health.
- `js/scienceui.js`: the Research Lab (Park management / Paleo-Flora / Paleo-Medicine tabs), GHOST, the Genetics Lab and the Botany Lab panels and their clicks. GHOST (`ghostHtml`): the drones (`droneCard`, one per drone with where it is, what it's after and a bar; `ghostLive` moves the bars from main.js; buy button), reports, the timeline (header `ghostHeadHtml`, map `ghostMapHtml`, slider `ghostTrackHtml`, site chips, site card `ghostCardHtml` with its Target / Genome / Survey Success table `targetRow`), Deep Time, and the trip log. Slider place `ghostU`, open site `ghostSite` and picked target `ghostPick` aren't saved. Dragging the slider, its keys and map taps (nearest marker within 14 map px) redraw the timeline's parts in place (`ghostRefresh`) so the slider keeps the pointer; card buttons go through the panel's click actions (`gsite`, `gpick`, `gscout`, `gdig`, `gdeep`, `gbuy`, `greport`). Styles are the `.gt-` rules in index.html.
- `js/logiui.js`: panels for stores, the dock, zones (the park office Staff tab's Work zones section, `zonesSectionHtml`: zone list, Draw a zone, supply lines toggle), and the park-office supply summary, plus their click/change handlers. Also defines `goodName`, which custodians.js uses.
- `js/guestui.js`: the park office's Guests section and the panels for food and gift shells (menu picker and prices), restrooms, bins, and seats.
- `js/main.js`: the rAF loop (`frame` → `step`), saving (localStorage plus the claude.ai `db` capability), and `startWith`.

Guests are drawn in `guestLayer`, above the whole world, so nothing in the world can cover them by depth. Two things stand in for that: `hidden34` hides a guest standing behind a building or vivarium tank (`occ34`), and `wallCut34` clips a guest standing behind a solid far-side exhibit fence or a barn, cave or burrow (`wall34`; fences: brick, stone, concrete, hedge and the like) so only what's above the fence's top edge shows. See-through fences, open canopies don't cover guests.

## GHOST timeline test page (tools/timeline)
The standalone prototype the game's GHOST panel was built from, NOT part of the game (its site block is still written by placesites.py, but its drones, reports and clock are its own): `tools/timeline/index.html` (opens from disk; the published copy is the same file with the `<!doctype>`/`<html>`/`<head>`/`<body>` wrapper stripped, plus `maps/*.png`). Twelve equal period bars, each split into equal epoch slices; inside a slice the years run on a log scale (`yearsAt`/`uAt`), with the Holocene's floor at 10 years. Labels round to two significant figures under a million years, then to millions (`fmtYearsAgo`). Maps are 260 x 130 px pictures in `tools/timeline/maps`, one a period with epoch overrides where the world changes a lot (Early/Late Jurassic, Early/Late Cretaceous, Pleistocene/Holocene; `MAPS`, `mapOf`), in one palette: navy rim, deep ocean, shallow sea, land, mountains, desert, ice. `tools/timeline/genmaps.py` drafts them from the PALEOMAP PaleoDEMs (Scotese & Wright 2018, CC BY 4.0, credited on the page; it needs numpy, h5py and Pillow and the unzipped 1-degree netCDF set, see its docstring): shelf above -200 m is shallow sea, the top 8% of land is mountains, deserts grow inland in the 20 to 30 degree dry belts, and ice is set by hand-written rules per map (the Pleistocene also drops the sea 120 m). The Late Cretaceous is drawn at 75 Ma so the Western Interior Seaway is open, and land needs a majority of a pixel's samples so narrow seas stay open. The drafts want a tidy in the pixel editor. The sites live in `tools/timeline/sites.py` (83 real localities, or historical ranges for the recently extinct, with modern latitude and longitude, a window in Ma and species ids; every one of the 142 species is at one or more). `tools/timeline/placesites.py` rotates each back with the PALEOMAP plate model (pygplates, from the PaleoAtlas for GPlates on Zenodo, CC BY 4.0) to the age of each map its window touches, takes species names, sizes and stars and each period's trip cost, days and risk from js/data.js, and rewrites the `SITES` block of index.html (between `SITES:BEGIN` and `SITES:END`; never edit it by hand). It stops if a species is at no site or a site holds a species of another period. It also nudges sites sharing a map apart (`spread`) until no two 6 px marker frames touch (centres `SEP` 7 px apart on an axis), costing up to about 5 px of accuracy in the crowded Late Cretaceous and Paleogene, and prints how far each map's markers moved. A tap goes to the nearest marker within `HIT_PX`; the selected marker's frame turns white; sites are drawn over them in code from `SITES` (pixel spot per map name, `from`/`to` years, fauna, trip cost, days, risk) and show for the whole of any epoch their window touches. A site starts dim and unnamed; the first GHOST scouting trip lights it and reveals its name. Scouting can fail: each animal still unseen there turns up with its own chance (`scoutChance`, `SCOUT.find` by size, so big animals are easier to spot), at most `SCOUT.max` (2) a trip, and the rest show as Undiscovered species rows until later trips find them. Once every animal has turned up the site reads Site Complete (`complete`: a badge on the card and its chip, a line in the report) and the scouting button goes away. An expedition targets one of those animals, and each row shows its genome % and its own Survey Success (`success`, the game's `tripOdds` maths: size from `TRIP.fail` plus the site's risk). Each trip ends in a post-mission overview (`report`): a scouting report lists "<animal> Discovered" for every animal no scouted site had before ("also found here" if one had), and an expedition report says "<animal> Sample Found" (plus "<animal> Genome Complete" on the find that fills it) or "Came back empty handed", while the Genome column updates. GHOST's drones (`DRONES`): every park starts with GHOST Mk. I, and the Research Lab researches Mk. II to V in order, which cut the empty-handed chance of scouting and expeditions by 15% to 50% of itself. The GHOST Drones menu (`renderFleet`) lists the fleet, 1 to `MAX_DRONES` (5) drones of the park's current Mk., bought with money at GHOST (they replace Temporal Researchers and aren't staff, so no wage or staff screen: the first comes with GHOST, then `dronePrice` is `DRONE_PRICE.base` (15,000) times `growth` (1.5) for each one owned past the first; the test page doesn't charge): where each is (site and epoch), what it is doing (scouting, or looking for an animal), a progress bar and when it gets back (`eta`, park-clock hours under a day). Each drone flies one mission at a time (`missions`, timed in park days on a test clock with a speed picker; a game day is 240 s at 1x), so a send needs a free drone, and a site takes one scouting trip and one expedition per target at a time. A mission resolves when it lands (`land`) and its report waits in the menu, and on its site's card, until dismissed. An animal can't be targeted until the park rating (a test picker) reaches its `stars`; each find adds `findGain` (2 to 8 trips a genome by size, as in the game). Scouted sites are per viewer in localStorage, a test convenience only.

## Paleopedia
The right column's Paleopedia button (`#animalsBtn`) opens `#dlgAnimals` (forest-green header bar). A row of forest-green tabs, one per period with launch-roster species and showing only the name (`ppPeriod`; clicking the open one closes it). An open tab shows just that period's species as text tiles: known ones (`ppKnown`: starter, discovered, or DNA collected) are buttons, the rest blank dashed slots, with "N of M discovered". Beside them is the card for the picked species (`ppCard`): sprite on a patch of its first biome's ground (`ppBg`/`ppGroundUrl`: the same 1 m tiles as the map, `sprites/ground/<biome>-<n>.png`; "No picture yet" when `SPRITES` has none), stats from `SPECIES`/`traitsOf`, `SPECIES_INFO` (data.js: `sci`, `ft`, `desc`), and an Acquire line from `speciesStatus`. Appeal stars are one per 5 points of `appeal` (1 to 25 in the data), 1 to 5 stars.
