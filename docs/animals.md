# Animals: keepers, food, escapes, power, wear, medicine, behavior

Moved out of CLAUDE.md. Update this file (not CLAUDE.md) when the code it describes changes.

- `js/keepers.js`: keepers (`hireKeeper`, `allStaffList`), food (`dailyNeed`, `shortages`, `eatTick`), CERES Paleoflora food output (`ceresTick`, `ceresRate`, `ceresCap`, `greenhouses`, `paleofloraReady`), cleaning, animal transfers (`requestMove`, `keeperMoveProblem`), depots and ATVs (`depots`, `syncAtvs`, `onAtv`), and the staff path graph (`buildKeeperGraph`, `walkFrom` Dijkstra). Call keeper / vet / mechanic (buttons in the exhibit panel's Keepers and food section; `CALLS` table, `callKeeper(e, kind)`, `state.staff.calls` {ex, keeper, kind}): the nearest free worker of that kind drops their job at the top of their tick loop (`takeCall`, `takeVetCall`, `takeMechCall`), unclaimed calls go to the next free one (`callJob`), and on arrival (`toCall`) a keeper hands over carried food, sets `c.focusEx` so `decide` feeds that exhibit first, and mucks it out if dirty; a vet runs a check-up then treats that exhibit's sick animals first (`focusEx`, `pickPatient(c, only)`); a mechanic inspects the fence and repairs it (maintenance.js). Escapes still come first. Vivarium dishes (`LAND.vdish`, a `tray` item with `vivToy`; `f.cap` is set from `VIV_DISH.cap[size]` when it's placed, `trayCap`): a vivarium holds no loose food (`storeMax` is 0), so keepers can only deliver into its dishes (`startFilling` with the keeper at the glass in front of the case, `vivFront`) and animals eat from them; no dish means `vivNoDish`/`dishText` ("Needs a food dish.", in the exhibit panel and the report), one dish serves `VIV_DISH.perDish` animals (`dishesNeeded`, `vivServed`: the rest go hungry in `eatTick`), and a dish holds one food. Old saves get one free dish per vivarium in `upgradeSave` (`s.vivDish`, loose `e.stock` moves into it). Food trays (`LAND` items with `tray`, `trays`, `trayRoomOf`, `trayShare`, `startFilling`/`fillStep`): a tray holds one food at a time, optionally fixed with `f.set` (picked in the exhibit's Habitat Props, Food Trays sub-tab, `propsTabs`); keepers with leftover food walk in and fill them, and `eatTick` drains trays before gate stock.
- `js/escapes.js`: barrier strength, breakouts, loose animals, guest deaths, viewing platforms (`viewFactor`), barrier upgrade costs (`upgradeCost`, `moatCost`, `aviaryCost`). Also home to a few shared helpers: `hasTech`, `isFlyer`, `nearestNode`.
- `js/power.js`: generators and electric-fence power allocation (`updatePower`).
- `js/maintenance.js`: fence/generator/depot wear, plus mechanics who inspect and repair.
- `js/medicine.js`: sickness and injuries (`illChance`, `injuryChance`, `healthNight`), the PMC ward (`admit`, `discharge`, `pmcRemoved`), vets who dart sick and escaped animals (`vetsTick`), and which medicine cures what (`canTreat`: the era's medicine plus a `ref-<Period>` refinement from ORACLE). CERES makes the doses in batches (science.js). Genome therapy (ORACLE tech `genetherapy`, `GENE` in data.js): the player queues a sickly clone (`a.gene`) from the exhibit's Health section, and a free vet (`pickGene`, jobs `toGene`/`splicing`, `geneDone`) lifts its `a.q` toward what the lab's genome gives a clone today (`labQuality` = `cloneQuality`, which GHOST raises by finding more of the genome), at most `GENE.step` a go, for `geneCost`. Also trash thrown into exhibits: `throwTick` (called from the guest loop in guests.js) lets rowdy parties, and others holding trash, throw it over an open exhibit's fence (`THROWN` in data.js; cut by working bins, guards via `guardNear`, and a `nofeed` sign within `THROWN.signReach`, `hasNoFeed`). `e.trash` counts pieces for the day, and `trashNight` (in `healthNight`) makes animals eat it: ill or dead (`trashEaten`).
- `js/behavior.js`: animal behavior, loaded after themes.js. Each animal carries six needs in `a.need` (`NEED_KEYS`: hunger, thirst, discomfort, lonely, bored, stress; 0 is met, 100 desperate) and an act `a.act` (`ACTS` in data.js: eat, drink, rest, patrol, hunt, forage, social, play, hide, pace) with `a.actFor` minutes and, when pacing, `a.why`. `behaviorTick` (from `tick`, every `BEHAVIOR.step` minutes) runs `needsStep` then `pickAct` per animal off a per-exhibit `behaviorCtx`; `behaviorNight` resets them in `endDay`. Vivarium animals never walk: when their act changes, `vivJump` (map.js) teleports them to the spot that act uses (`actGoal`: dish, water, hide or plant), or back to their home spot (where they first appeared) if the act has none. Species traits (`traitsOf`: `active` diurnal/nocturnal/crepuscular via `awake`, `social` solitary/pair/herd/pack via `matesWanted`, `temper` skittish/curious/aggressive) come from `TRAITS` in data.js, else from diet, group and size. Dev-mode gating (`toyHidden`): with `devMode()` off, open-exhibit toy props (`HIDDEN_TOYS`) are hidden from Habitat Props and open exhibits always have full enrichment (`enrichment(e)` is 1); vivarium props (all `vivToy` items, including Misters) are available to everyone, and `enrichment(e)` is real inside vivariums, with `ENRICH.vivBase` (.2) added for non-dev players so a bare vivarium is not harsh on day one; the vivarium panel hints "Add enrichment" when toys fall short of `enrichNeed`. Dev mode is unchanged (no baseline, everything counts). Enrichment (`enrichment`, `enrichNeed`, `toyPoints`; `ENRICH`, and the toy props in `LAND`, under Habitat Props' Enrichment sub-tab: `post`/`logs`/`wallow`/`ball` with flat `toy` points, and `icefish`/`icefruit`/`shank`/`buglog`/`hay` with `toyFor` points by diet (`toyFor(t, s)`, `toyPoints(e, s)`, `toyFit` scores each species against `enrichNeedOf`). Items with `lasts` (frozen treats, hay bales) count down `f.left` in `treatsNight` and vanish, frozen ones `ENRICH.melt` faster in a heat wave; hay bales also feed plant eaters (`hay` in `browseRate`), and Paleoflora bales (`pbale`) feed the older ones (`paleo`), taking `ceres` Paleoflora from `state.ceres.stock` in `placeLand` and needing the `paleoflora` tech; vivariums have their own `vivToy` items, `vbark`/`vbranch`/`vdig`/`vfeed`/`vmist`, picked from a dropdown in the vivarium's Exhibit tab, `vivToysHtml`, and set in the glass by `addVivToy`/`vivToyProblem` in landscape.js) slows boredom and lets them play. Midday naps (`BEHAVIOR.nap`, `napLull`, `napRoll`, `a.napFor`/`a.napDay` in `pickAct`): a settled animal awake by day lies down once a day for 30 to 80 minutes, most likely around 13:00 and more in a heat wave, and stays down until the nap is over; `actGoal`'s rest case sends it to the nearest shelter or tree. Attacks (end of behavior.js, `ATTACK` in data.js): `attackStep(e, ctx)` runs each behavior step. A predator (or carnivore, per `attackReason`) with prey in the exhibit gets the `hunt` act (`preyIn`, a pull in `pickAct`) only while `huntDriven` (hunger, stress or boredom over the `ATTACK` lines; a fed, calm one ignores prey). Buying, cloning, placing or moving an animal into an exhibit where someone hunts someone asks first (`mixWarning`, a confirm dialog in ui.js), map.js `actGoal` walks it up to the nearest prey, and once it has stalked `ATTACK.stalk` minutes and is within `ATTACK.strike` it bites; stressed aggressive (or crowded) animals fight. `looseAttacks` (from `escapesTick`) lets a loose predator within `ATTACK.reach` of an exhibit it can get into (`canGetIn`: breached, or stronger than the fence) attack. `hurtAnimal` adds an injury (`a.sick.hit` {by, how}, which `animalDies` turns into the death-dialog cause), fires the dust fx, and `a.hitAt`/`l.biteAt` space attacks by `ATTACK.gap`. Effects: `behaviorScore` in `exhibitReport` (stress, boredom, the Pacing/Hiding tells), `liveliness` on appeal in `recompute`, `stressEscape` in `escapesTick`, `BEHAVIOR.stressIll` in `illChance`, and the `pacing`/`playful` guest thoughts in `seeExhibit`. map.js walks each act (`animalPlan`, `actGoal`, `paceRoute`, `patrolRoute`, `ACT_GAIT`): pacing animals get an amber dashed ring and hiding ones fade. The exhibit panel's Behavior tab is `behaviorHtml` in ui.js. Going inside (behavior.js "going inside", `SHELTER` in data.js): `shelterStep` runs after `pickAct` each step and sends an animal to the nearest shelter (`LAND` item with `slots`, via `shelterFits`/`shelterTake`, capacity tracked per step in `ctx.load`) to hide (act `hide`), sleep (resting while `awake` is at or under `SHELTER.sleepBelow`, or on a nap; the park being shut counts as night for day animals in `awake`) or wait out bad weather (storm, heat wave, cold snap, a share of animals per day, `shelterWhy`). `shelterRun` (animal id to {f, why, in, since, take}) is runtime only; `a.inside` ("hide"/"sleep"/"weather") is set once it arrives (`shelterArrive`, also called per frame by `animateAnimals`), cleared when the reason ends, every night and by `upgradeSave`. map.js: `actGoal` walks it to its shelter, `animateAnimals` hides the element while `a.inside` and skips it, `animalAt`/`drawAnimalRing` ignore it, `actLabel` says "Sheltering from the storm"/"Sleeping inside"/"Hiding inside". If every shelter is full it stays outside. Guests: `visibleShare(e)` (hiding counts fully, sleeping inside by day half and never at night, weather a quarter, `HIDING.weights`) is averaged in `hideAvg` over `HIDING.window`; `hideBother(p, e)` (only past `HIDING.from`, divided by `pm(p, "hideTol")`, cut by `p.edu`) trims mood and the visit's pleasure in `seeExhibit` and adds the "hiding" thought, and `behaviorScore`/`behaviorHtml` show the hint. Privacy Screens (`LAND.screen`, `SCREEN`, `screenShare`, drawn by `screen34`) and Quiet Signs (`BUILDINGS.quiet`, `QUIET`, `quietSigns`, `guestsSeen`) both cut `ctx.seen`, the crowd skittish animals feel.
- Animal sex: every animal has `a.sex` ("male"/"female"; `SEX` in data.js; `pickSex`, `sexMark`, `sexSplit` in sim.js; `nameAnimal` assigns one if missing (and `a.face`, 1 right or -1 left, random: the way its picture first faces, which is what a vivarium animal keeps), `upgradeSave` migrates, and copies of animals must carry `sex` like `name`). TAR clone jobs carry it (`orderClone(sp, exhibitId, sex)`; Clone M / Clone F buttons). Effects: `singleSexKinds` (behavior.js) makes pair/herd species with one sex lonelier (`SEX.lonelyMult`) and less happy (`SEX.happy`); `fightBias(a)` is the male-aggression hook for animal attacks. No breeding yet. Social system (behavior.js "social life", `SOCIAL` in data.js; open exhibits only, vivariums ignore it): `a.rank` (saved, 0 alpha to 1 bottom; `pickRank`, `byRank`, `rankPos`, shown in the animal panel) orders herds and packs. `socialInfo(e)` reads each species' `traitsOf(s).social` and the sexes: herd/pack all-male or all-female are stable and happy, one male with females is stable, two or more males fight (`rivalFight` from `attackStep`, via `hurtAnimal`, so injuries and dust; loser stressed, lowest rank worst, scaled by males and room), loners are stressed by company (same sex worst, eased by room, rocks and shelter), pairs want one male and one female. `socialStress` feeds `stressTarget`, `behaviorScore` adds the happiness and a "Social." line, and the Behavior tab lists it. Packs (`traitsOf(s).social === "pack"`, `PACK` in data.js) skip the herd rules: `packOrder` puts the alpha pair (best rank plus the best of the other sex) first and `socialInfo` returns a `pack` entry (too small, no alpha pair, short of food; also for one animal alone); `eatTick` (keepers.js) fills `packFeed` so shortages hit subordinates first (`needsStep` hunger); mates join a hunt in `pickAct` and `packHuntBoost` raises the strike chance; `packChallenge` (from `attackStep`) is a rare, stress-driven rank swap. Vivariums ignore all of it.
- `js/animalui.js`: individual animals. Every animal has `a.name` (default species id uppercased + 3-digit counter, e.g. `COTY-001`; `nameAnimal(a)` in sim.js, counter `state.animalSeq` so numbers are never reused; `upgradeSave` names old animals; every place that copies an animal object (escapes, holding, ward, cloning) must carry `name`). Tapping an animal in select mode (`animalAt`) opens `sel = {kind:"animal"}`: `animalHtml`/`animalLive` (editable name, max 24 chars, mood, needs, wants, stressors), ringed by `drawAnimalRing`.
