# Animals: keepers, food, escapes, power, wear, medicine, behavior

Moved out of CLAUDE.md. Update this file (not CLAUDE.md) when the code it describes changes.

- `js/keepers.js`: keepers (`hireKeeper`, `allStaffList`), food (`dailyNeed`, `shortages`, `eatTick`), Botany Lab Paleoflora food output (`ceresTick`, `ceresRate`, `ceresCap`, `greenhouses`, `paleofloraReady`), cleaning, animal transfers (`requestMove`, `keeperMoveProblem`), depots and ATVs (`depots`, `syncAtvs`, `onAtv`), and the staff path graph (`buildKeeperGraph`, `walkFrom` Dijkstra). Call keeper / vet / mechanic (buttons in the exhibit panel's Keepers and food section; `CALLS` table, `callKeeper(e, kind)`, `state.staff.calls` {ex, keeper, kind}): the nearest free worker of that kind drops their job at the top of their tick loop (`takeCall`, `takeVetCall`, `takeMechCall`), unclaimed calls go to the next free one (`callJob`), and on arrival (`toCall`) a keeper hands over carried food, sets `c.focusEx` so `decide` feeds that exhibit first, and mucks it out if dirty; a vet runs a check-up then treats that exhibit's sick animals first (`focusEx`, `pickPatient(c, only)`); a mechanic inspects the fence and repairs it (maintenance.js). Escapes still come first. Vivarium dishes (`LAND.vdish`, a `tray` item with `vivToy`; `f.cap` is set from `VIV_DISH.cap[size]` when it's placed, `trayCap`): a vivarium holds no loose food (`storeMax` is 0), so keepers can only deliver into its dishes (`startFilling` with the keeper at the glass in front of the case, `vivFront`) and animals eat from them; no dish means `vivNoDish`/`dishText` ("Needs a food dish.", in the exhibit panel and the report), one dish serves `VIV_DISH.perDish` animals (`dishesNeeded`, `vivServed`: the rest go hungry in `eatTick`), and a dish holds one food. Old saves get one free dish per vivarium in `upgradeSave` (`s.vivDish`, loose `e.stock` moves into it). Food trays (`LAND` items with `tray`, `trays`, `trayRoomOf`, `trayShare`, `startFilling`/`fillStep`): a tray holds one food at a time, optionally fixed with `f.set` (picked in the exhibit's Habitat Props, Food Trays sub-tab, `propsTabs`); keepers with leftover food walk in and fill them, and `eatTick` drains trays before gate stock.
- `js/escapes.js`: barrier strength, breakouts, loose animals, guest deaths, viewing platforms (`viewFactor`), barrier upgrade costs (`upgradeCost`, `moatCost`, `aviaryCost`). Also home to a few shared helpers: `hasTech`, `isFlyer`, `nearestNode`.
- `js/power.js`: generators, and power for electric fences (strongest animals first), then aquarium filters (`filterKw`, sets `e.filterPowered`; biggest load first), then cold stores (`updatePower`).
- `js/maintenance.js`: fence/generator/depot wear, plus mechanics who inspect and repair.
- `js/medicine.js`: sickness and injuries (`illChance`, `injuryChance`, `healthNight`), the PMC ward (`admit`, `discharge`, `pmcRemoved`), vets who dart sick and escaped animals (`vetsTick`), and which medicine cures what (`canTreat`: the era's medicine plus a `ref-<Period>` refinement from the Research Lab). The Botany Lab makes the doses in batches (science.js). Genome therapy (Research Lab tech `genetherapy`, `GENE` in data.js): an exhibit toggle (`e.geneTherapy`, button in the Health tab, `geneProblem` says why it can't be switched on). While it is on, each vet check-up (`checkDone`, so called ones too) runs `geneTherapy(e, who)` on `geneTargets(e)`, the animals the lab's genome can improve by `GENE.minGain`: it lifts `a.q` toward what the lab's genome gives a clone today (`labQuality` = `cloneQuality`, which the Time Travel Lab raises by finding more of the genome), at most `GENE.step` a go, for `geneCost` each while the money lasts, and `checkWork(e)` makes the check-up take `GENE.minutes` longer per animal. Also trash thrown into exhibits: `throwTick` (called from the guest loop in guests.js) lets rowdy parties, and others holding trash, throw it over an open exhibit's fence (`THROWN` in data.js; cut by working bins, guards via `guardNear`, and a `nofeed` sign within `THROWN.signReach`, `hasNoFeed`). `e.trash` counts pieces for the day, and `trashNight` (in `healthNight`) makes animals eat it: ill or dead (`trashEaten`).
- `js/behavior.js`: animal behavior, loaded after themes.js. Each animal carries six needs in `a.need` (`NEED_KEYS`: hunger, thirst, discomfort, lonely, bored, stress; 0 is met, 100 desperate) and an act `a.act` (`ACTS` in data.js: eat, drink, rest, patrol, hunt, forage, social, play, hide, pace) with `a.actFor` minutes and, when pacing, `a.why`. `behaviorTick` (from `tick`, every `BEHAVIOR.step` minutes) runs `needsStep` then `pickAct` per animal off a per-exhibit `behaviorCtx`; `behaviorNight` resets them in `endDay`. Vivarium animals never walk: when their act changes, `vivJump` (map.js) teleports them to the spot that act uses (`actGoal`: dish, water, hide or plant), or back to their home spot (where they first appeared) if the act has none. A goal or home that fails `vivSpot` is pulled toward the middle of the case (`vivPull`); a home with no lamp or pool is drawn from the middle third of the case's depth (`vivMidSpot`, the candidate furthest from the other animals and the fittings), and `quietSpot` in a case is just its middle. Pictures never overlap in a case: `vivBody` is an animal's picture room (half its width, half its body height), `vivCrowd(h, p)` tests a spot against the others and against the bases of tall fittings (an animal is drawn over fittings, so one standing behind a base would hover over it), new homes skip crowded spots and `vivClear` moves a jump's landing spot to the nearest clear one. `place34` draws a case's animals over all its fittings, north to south among themselves. Species traits (`traitsOf`: `active` diurnal/nocturnal/crepuscular via `awake`, `social` solitary/pair/herd/pack via `matesWanted`, `temper` skittish/curious/aggressive) come from `TRAITS` in data.js, else from diet, group and size. Dev-mode gating (`toyHidden`): with `devMode()` off, open-exhibit toy props (`HIDDEN_TOYS`) are hidden from Habitat Props and open exhibits always have full enrichment (`enrichment(e)` is 1); vivarium props (all `vivToy` items, including Misters) are available to everyone, and `enrichment(e)` is real inside vivariums, with `ENRICH.vivBase` (.2) added for non-dev players so a bare vivarium is not harsh on day one; the vivarium panel shows toy points against `enrichNeed` on a meter. Dev mode is unchanged (no baseline, everything counts). Enrichment (`enrichment`, `enrichNeed`, `toyPoints`; `ENRICH`, and the toy props in `LAND`, under Habitat Props' Enrichment sub-tab: `post`/`logs`/`wallow`/`ball` with flat `toy` points, and `icefish`/`icefruit`/`shank`/`buglog`/`hay` with `toyFor` points by diet (`toyFor(t, s)`, `toyPoints(e, s)`, `toyFit` scores each species against `enrichNeedOf`). Items with `lasts` (frozen treats, hay bales) count down `f.left` in `treatsNight` and vanish, frozen ones `ENRICH.melt` faster in a heat wave; hay bales also feed plant eaters (`hay` in `browseRate`), and Paleoflora bales (`pbale`) feed the older ones (`paleo`), taking `ceres` Paleoflora from `state.ceres.stock` in `placeLand` and needing the `paleoflora` tech; vivariums have their own `vivToy` items, `vbark`/`vbranch`/`vdig`/`vfeed`/`vmist`, picked from a dropdown in the vivarium's Exhibit tab, `vivToysHtml`, and set in the glass by `addVivToy`/`vivToyProblem` in landscape.js) slows boredom and lets them play. Midday naps (`BEHAVIOR.nap`, `napLull`, `napRoll`, `a.napFor`/`a.napDay` in `pickAct`): a settled animal awake by day lies down once a day for 30 to 80 minutes, most likely around 13:00 and more in a heat wave, and stays down until the nap is over; `actGoal`'s rest case sends it to the nearest shelter or tree. Attacks (end of behavior.js, `ATTACK` in data.js): `attackStep(e, ctx)` runs each behavior step. A predator (or carnivore, per `attackReason`) with prey in the exhibit gets the `hunt` act (`preyIn`, a pull in `pickAct`) only while `huntDriven` (hunger, stress or boredom over the `ATTACK` lines; a fed, calm one ignores prey). Buying, cloning, placing or moving an animal into an exhibit where someone hunts someone asks first (`mixWarning`, a confirm dialog in ui.js), map.js `actGoal` walks it up to the nearest prey, and once it has stalked `ATTACK.stalk` minutes and is within `ATTACK.strike` it bites; stressed aggressive (or crowded) animals fight. `looseAttacks` (from `escapesTick`) lets a loose predator within `ATTACK.reach` of an exhibit it can get into (`canGetIn`: breached, or stronger than the fence) attack. `hurtAnimal` adds an injury (`a.sick.hit` {by, how}, which `animalDies` turns into the death-dialog cause), fires the dust fx, and `a.hitAt`/`l.biteAt` space attacks by `ATTACK.gap`. Effects: `behaviorScore` in `exhibitReport` (stress, boredom, the Pacing/Hiding tells), `liveliness` on appeal in `recompute`, `stressEscape` in `escapesTick`, `BEHAVIOR.stressIll` in `illChance`, and the `pacing`/`playful` guest thoughts in `seeExhibit`. map.js walks each act (`animalPlan`, `actGoal`, `paceRoute`, `patrolRoute`, `ACT_GAIT`): pacing animals get an amber dashed ring and hiding ones fade. The exhibit panel's Behavior tab is `behaviorHtml` in ui.js. Going inside (behavior.js "going inside", `SHELTER` in data.js): `shelterStep` runs after `pickAct` each step and sends an animal to the nearest shelter (`LAND` item with `slots`, via `shelterFits`/`shelterTake`, capacity tracked per step in `ctx.load`) to hide (act `hide`), sleep (resting while `awake` is at or under `SHELTER.sleepBelow`, or on a nap; the park being shut counts as night for day animals in `awake`) or wait out bad weather (storm, heat wave, cold snap, a share of animals per day, `shelterWhy`). `shelterRun` (animal id to {f, why, in, since, take}) is runtime only; `a.inside` ("hide"/"sleep"/"weather") is set once it arrives (`shelterArrive`, also called per frame by `animateAnimals`), cleared when the reason ends, every night and by `upgradeSave`. map.js: `actGoal` walks it to its shelter, `animateAnimals` hides the element while `a.inside` and skips it, `animalAt`/`drawAnimalRing` ignore it, `actLabel` says "Sheltering from the storm"/"Sleeping inside"/"Hiding inside". If every shelter is full it stays outside. Guests: `visibleShare(e)` (hiding counts fully, sleeping inside by day half and never at night, weather a quarter, `HIDING.weights`) is averaged in `hideAvg` over `HIDING.window`; `hideBother(p, e)` (only past `HIDING.from`, divided by `pm(p, "hideTol")`, cut by `p.edu`) trims mood and the visit's pleasure in `seeExhibit` and adds the "hiding" thought, and `behaviorScore`/`behaviorHtml` show the hint. Privacy Screens (`LAND.screen`, `SCREEN`, `screenShare`, drawn by `screen34`) and Quiet Signs (`BUILDINGS.quiet`, `QUIET`, `quietSigns`, `guestsSeen`) both cut `ctx.seen`, the crowd skittish animals feel. Land animals keep out of exhibit water (`walkArea`'s `wat`); one found standing in a pond (water drawn over it, or a spawn with no dry spot) is walked out by `wadeOut` (map.js) at its next plan.
- Animal sex: every animal has `a.sex` ("male"/"female"; `SEX` in data.js; `pickSex`, `sexMark`, `sexSplit` in sim.js; `nameAnimal` assigns one if missing (and `a.face`, 1 right or -1 left, random: the way its picture first faces, which is what a vivarium animal keeps), `upgradeSave` migrates, and copies of animals must carry `sex` like `name`). Genetics Lab clone jobs carry it (`orderClone(sp, exhibitId, sex)`; Clone M / Clone F buttons). Effects: `singleSexKinds` (behavior.js) makes pair/herd species with one sex lonelier (`SEX.lonelyMult`) and less happy (`SEX.happy`); `fightBias(a)` is the male-aggression hook for animal attacks. No breeding yet. Social system (behavior.js "social life", `SOCIAL` in data.js; open exhibits only, vivariums ignore it): `a.rank` (saved, 0 alpha to 1 bottom; `pickRank`, `byRank`, `rankPos`, shown in the animal panel) orders herds and packs. `socialInfo(e)` reads each species' `traitsOf(s).social` and the sexes: herd/pack all-male or all-female are stable and happy, one male with females is stable, two or more males fight (`rivalFight` from `attackStep`, via `hurtAnimal`, so injuries and dust; loser stressed, lowest rank worst, scaled by males and room), loners are stressed by company (same sex worst, eased by room, rocks and shelter), pairs want one male and one female. `socialStress` feeds `stressTarget`, `behaviorScore` adds the happiness and a "Social." line, and the Behavior tab lists it. Packs (`traitsOf(s).social === "pack"`, `PACK` in data.js) skip the herd rules: `packOrder` puts the alpha pair (best rank plus the best of the other sex) first and `socialInfo` returns a `pack` entry (too small, no alpha pair, short of food; also for one animal alone); `eatTick` (keepers.js) fills `packFeed` so shortages hit subordinates first (`needsStep` hunger); mates join a hunt in `pickAct` and `packHuntBoost` raises the strike chance; `packChallenge` (from `attackStep`) is a rare, stress-driven rank swap. Vivariums ignore all of it.
- `js/animalui.js`: individual animals. Every animal has `a.name` (default species id uppercased + 3-digit counter, e.g. `COTY-001`; `nameAnimal(a)` in sim.js, counter `state.animalSeq` so numbers are never reused; `upgradeSave` names old animals; every place that copies an animal object (escapes, holding, ward, cloning) must carry `name`). Tapping an animal in select mode (`animalAt`) opens `sel = {kind:"animal"}`: `animalHtml`/`animalLive` (editable name, max 24 chars, mood, needs, Health, wants, stressors). The Health section opens with a 0 to 100% health bar, `animalHp(a)` (medicine.js: 100 minus the sickness's severity, full while it's well or its illness is still hidden; `hpColor` picks green, yellow or red), the same bar each animal gets in the exhibit's Animals tab (`animalsTabHtml`: every animal by name with its own Move (the move dialog for just that one, `requestMove`'s `id`; Moving while queued) and Sell, then waiting moves; the Exhibit tab's "Animals here" only lists counts) and in the Health tab's list of every animal, worst first. Below it is `animalHealthItems(a, e, s)`: condition and severity (injury cause from `a.sick.hit`/`cause`, `sickStatus`; hidden illness never shows), DNA quality, medicated today, the exhibit's last vet check-up. `basicsLow(e, list)` (medicine.js) adds low food stock, hungry, no water, thirsty and low sanitation lines (only when low) to both that section and the exhibit's Health tab, ringed by `drawAnimalRing`.
- Land animals and water (map.js): only `semi` species (and flyers) may enter exhibit water. `walkArea` gives everyone else `wat` plus `wm` (half a body), so `inWater`/`walkClear` treat a pond as its polygon grown by `wm`; `wadeOut` hops a stranded animal to the bank rather than routing across; the step loop in `animateAnimals` refuses any step that goes into water (`landWet`).
- Crates (science.js, `CRATE` in data.js), Zoo Tycoon style: an animal boxed off the map turns into a crate standing in the park, `state.crates` (`crates()`): the animal's record (name, sex, rank, DNA quality, clone flag, illness) plus `x`, `y`. There is no holding list or fee. A crate is in no exhibit, so nothing feeds, ages or treats it and guests never see it, until `uncrate(id, e)` opens it into an exhibit (`crateProblem(r, e)` says why it can't in a few words, `fitsHabitat`; a pool animal also wants its water in, `putAnimal` brings it back whole). `crateAnimal(e, a)` is instant and leaves the crate where the animal stood (`bodyPos`); the Crate button on the animal panel and the Animals tab (`crateAnimalNow` in animalui.js), pool animals whose exhibit loses its lagoon wall or all its water (`crateStranded`, run first in `afterChange`) and the pool animals of a bulldozed exhibit or gate (`removeItem` > `releaseAnimals` in map.js; every other animal there gets out through `turnLoose` in escapes.js, with no rating hit) all use it. A recaptured escapee where it was caught, and a moved or discharged animal with nowhere to go at its old exhibit or the PMC (`dropCrate(a, x, y)`, `labSpot`). Finished clones are not crates, see Specimens below. A crate stands on free ground only (`crateSpotProblem`: inside the park, not on a building, path, fence, case, water, another crate or within `CRATE.entrance` m of the entrance; inside an open exhibit is fine), and `crateSpotNear` finds the nearest free spot in rings. `moveCrate(id, x, y)` sets it down elsewhere and `sellCrate(id)` sells it at `COST.animalResale`. A crate is the picture `sprites/crate.png` (`tools/pixelcrate.py`) scaled to the animal (`crateScale`: its `animalRadius` over `CRATE.perRadius`, from `CRATE.min` to `CRATE.max`). Old saves' `science.ready` become crates with no spot (`x` null) and `settleCrates` puts them by the lab. The UI is in docs/ui.md.
- Specimens and deliveries (science.js, keepers.js): a finished clone joins `state.science.specimens` (`specimens()`), the animal's record, and waits at the Genetics Lab; the lab panel's Specimens tab (`specimensHtml`, scienceui.js) lists them with where each is headed, who has it, and Move (`openSpecimenDialog` in ui.js picks an exhibit it fits) or Cancel (`cancelDelivery`, before pickup). A clone ordered for an exhibit queues its delivery on completion (`finishClone`). `deliverSpecimen(id, exhibitId)` checks `crateProblem`, a connected lab (the `tar` stop on the staff graph) and a keeper gate, then adds a transfer `{lab:true, from:lab id, to:exhibit id}` to `state.staff.transfers` and hands it to the nearest idle keeper, custodian or vet (`assignDelivery`); if nobody is idle, the first to look for work takes it (`deliveryJob`, in `decide`, `custDecide`, `vetDecide`, after hunts and calls, before chores, patients and check-ups). The worker walks to the lab (`toPickup`), carries the crate (`c.crate`, drawn small in the hands by `carryCrate` in map.js) and drops it at the gate (`toDropoff`); `dropOff` puts the animal in with `putAnimal`, or back on the list if the exhibit can't take it. The specimen stays listed until it's in. Keepers use `arrive`; vets and custodians use `labPickup`/`labDrop`. At the 4 AM rollover a carried specimen is delivered at once (`keepersNight`), a firing finishes it off (`fireStaff`), and a delivery whose exhibit or lab is gone is dropped (`deliveryTidy`, in `afterChange`).
