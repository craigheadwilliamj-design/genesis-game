# Animals: keepers, food, escapes, power, wear, medicine, behavior

Moved out of CLAUDE.md. Update this file (not CLAUDE.md) when the code it describes changes.

- `js/keepers.js`: keepers (`hireKeeper`, `allStaffList`), food (`dailyNeed`, `shortages`, `eatTick`), Botany Lab Paleoflora food output (`ceresTick`, `ceresRate`, `ceresCap`, `greenhouses`, `paleofloraReady`), cleaning, animal transfers (`requestMove`, `keeperMoveProblem`), depots and ATVs (`depots`, `syncAtvs`, `onAtv`), and the staff path graph (`buildKeeperGraph`, `walkFrom` Dijkstra). Call keeper / vet / mechanic (and Call custodian on stands, shops and hotels, see docs/guests.md) (buttons in the exhibit panel's Keepers and food section; `CALLS` table, `callKeeper(e, kind)`, `state.staff.calls` {ex, keeper, kind}): the nearest free worker of that kind drops their job at the top of their tick loop (`takeCall`, `takeVetCall`, `takeMechCall`), unclaimed calls go to the next free one (`callJob`), and on arrival (`toCall`) a keeper hands over carried food, sets `c.focusEx` so `decide` feeds that exhibit first, and mucks it out if dirty; a vet runs a check-up then treats that exhibit's sick animals first (`focusEx`, `pickPatient(c, only)`); a mechanic inspects the fence and repairs it (maintenance.js). Escapes still come first. Vivarium dishes (`LAND.vdish`, a `tray` item with `vivToy`; `f.cap` is set from `VIV_DISH.cap[size]` when it's placed, `trayCap`): a vivarium holds no loose food (`storeMax` is 0), so keepers can only deliver into its dishes (`startFilling` with the keeper at the glass in front of the case, `vivFront`) and animals eat from them; no dish means `vivNoDish`/`dishText` ("Needs a food dish.", in the exhibit panel and the report), one dish serves `VIV_DISH.perDish` animals (`dishesNeeded`, `vivServed`: the rest go hungry in `eatTick`), and a dish holds one food. Old saves get one free dish per vivarium in `upgradeSave` (`s.vivDish`, loose `e.stock` moves into it). Food trays (`LAND` items with `tray`, `trays`, `trayRoomOf`, `trayShare`, `startFilling`/`fillStep`): a tray holds one food at a time, optionally fixed with `f.set` (picked in the exhibit's Habitat Props, Food Trays sub-tab, `propsTabs`); keepers with leftover food walk in and fill them, and `eatTick` drains trays before gate stock.
- Aquarists (keepers.js, `AQUARIST` in data.js, same hire cost and wage as keepers): a keeper record with `role:"aqua"` (`isAqua`), so crew, carry and ATVs are shared. Their building is the Dive Shack (`BUILDINGS.diveshack`, a copy of the Keeper Station with the same store; `stations(c)` gives keepers stations and aquarists shacks, hiring one needs its own building); `crew` entries mirror `role` (`aquaCrew`, set in `syncCrew`). Lagoons and aquariums (`aquaEx(e)`: `e.aqua` or `lagoonWalled`) are tended only by aquarists and every other exhibit only by keepers (`tends(k, e)`), in `shortages`, `cleanJob`, `moveJob`/`assignDelivery` (a move or delivery counts as aqua if either end is) and calls (`CALLS.aqua`, "Call aquarist"). A lagoon holds pool animals only (`fitsHabitat`), so `aquaEx` never covers land animals; an old save's land animal in a lagoon just gets a bad report line. Aquarists never hunt escapes (`huntJob`) or drop mucking for one; keepers do. Vets and custodians are not split. `STAFF_TYPES.aquarist` filters the keepers list by role (`store` is the real list for firing); zone panels list them in their own section. Aquarists are drawn with the keeper sprite for now.
- `js/escapes.js`: barrier strength, breakouts, loose animals, guest deaths, viewing platforms (`viewFactor`), barrier upgrade costs (`upgradeCost`, `moatCost`, `aviaryCost`). Also home to a few shared helpers: `hasTech`, `isFlyer`, `nearestNode`.
- `js/power.js`: the power grid (`POWER` in data.js). Power stations are buildings with `power` (kW) in `BUILDINGS`: the Diesel Generator (`generator`, no research), and `gasplant`, `solar`, `wind`, `geothermal` behind TECH of the same ids. Each has `reach` (meters from its middle), `eyesore` (0 for green ones), `green`, and `wear` (times `POWER.genWear`); `isStation`, `generators()` (every station), `genOnline`, `stationShare`/`stationOutput` (solar follows `darkLevel()` and `POWER.sun` by the sky or event now (`wxNow`), wind `POWER.wind` by the event). Transformers (`BUILDINGS.transformer`, `grid:true`, `noPath:true` so they count as reached with no path) only carry power: `isGridNode` is a station or a transformer. Stations and transformers both wear (`gridMachines()`, `wear` times `POWER.genWear` a day, in `wearTick`) and are mechanics' machines (`isMachine`, `isDown`, `repairCost`); a mechanic needs a path or road beside one to reach it (its `kGraph` anchor). Below `POWER.offlineBelow` a station makes nothing but still passes power on, while a transformer drops out of `gridNets` entirely, so it can split a network. Cables never wear. Bad weather can knock one out (`POWER.hit`): `rollWeather` calls `rollGridHit`, which on a storm, snow or sandstorm day may set `state.weather.hit = {at}` (a park minute); `gridHitTick` (from `powerTick`) lands it once the clock passes, on one working station or transformer picked by `POWER.hit.weight` (solar more in a sandstorm), leaving it at `POWER.hit.cond`, offline until a mechanic repairs it.
  - Cables: `state.cables` [{id, a, b}] join two grid nodes by building id, straight and underground (`layCable`, `cableProblem`, `cableCost` at `POWER.cablePerMeter`, at most `POWER.cableMax` m; `liftCable` refunds `COST.refundShare`; `dropCables(id)` when a node is bulldozed, from `removeItem`). `gridNets()` unions the nodes into networks with `supply` (now) and `cap` (best).
  - `updatePower` (from `recompute`, so every 20 park minutes and on every change) hands each network's supply out, most important first: electric fences by the strongest animal (`fenceDraw`), aquarium filters by load (`filterKw`, `e.filterPowered`), cold stores, buildings with their own `kw` (labs, PMC, Education Center, resort), guest buildings and tram stations, then the rest. Every building but path props, decks/galleries and the grid itself draws (`needsPower`, `kwOf`: `t.kw` or `POWER.perSqM` a m²) and gets `b.powered`; `unpowered(b)` is the test everything else uses. A user is fed by a network one of whose nodes `reaches` it (an exhibit needs only one stretch of fence in reach); with several, the one with the most left. `gridInfo` keeps the last result (`nets`, `netOf` node id → net, `feeds` user id → net) for panels and the map. A tram station flipping power marks `gridStale`, and `powerTick` (each step) rebuilds the guest graph.
  - What no power does: `servesOf` gives nothing (stands, shops, restrooms), the Education Center drops out of `pickSight`, `hotels()`, `offices()` (cameras), `greenhouses()` and farms' `makes` skip it, `dept()` returns null and `deptProblem` says "has no power" (labs; the Research Lab's projects are held, Genetics Lab clones and Botany beds already pause via `dept`), the PMC ward stops, tram stations leave the guest graph, break rooms don't count for rest or morale, and any unpowered backstage building costs staff morale. The map puts a bolt over each (render, next to the low-stock "!").
  - Older parks: `upgradeSave` adds `cables` and gives `state.powerFree` (day) of `POWER.grace` days in which `powerFree()` powers everything; the `generator` tech is refunded. New parks have `cables:[]` and no grace.
  - Guests: `indexGrid(nodes)` (from `buildGuestGraph`) sets `n.eyesore` and `n.green` (0 to 1) on each guest node from grid nodes within `POWER.sightReach`; `gridMood(p)` in `guestsTick` costs `POWER.eyesoreHurt` (times the `eyesore` personality fx: conservationists 1.5) and gives conservationists `POWER.greenJoy`, with the thoughts `eyesore` and `greenPower`. A guest with no stop for a need also thinks `noPower` when some guest building is dark.
- `js/maintenance.js`: fence/power station/transformer/depot wear, plus mechanics who inspect and repair.
- `js/medicine.js`: sickness and injuries. Every case is a condition (`CONDITIONS` in data.js, `a.sick = {kind, name, sev, cause, hidden, hit}`) of one of three kinds (`SICK_KIND`: injury, infection, poison, each with its start, worsen, hidden and on-the-spot numbers) in a severity band (`SICK_BAND`, `sickBand`: Mild, Serious, Critical, which slow the animal, make it rest, eat less and stress, and weigh on happiness and guests). `bodyTags`/`bodyOf`/`canGet` say which bodies get which condition (`CLADE`, `GORES`, `LIMBLESS`, `TOOTHLESS`, `SHELLED`; `aquatic`, Tiktaalik counts as a fish), `illChance`/`injuryChance` give a day's chance with the causes raising it (`why`, weights `w`: dirt above all, foul water, hunger, thirst, stress, crowding, lack of shelter, no heat lamp; rivals, predators, weather, dirty or wrong ground), and `pickCause` then `pickCondition(s, kinds, causes)` name the case (`injuryCauses` adds gore for gorers). `healthTick` runs `healthStep` every `HEALTH.step` park minutes through the day: new cases (`newCase`, `fallSick(a, id, cause, sev)`; trash eaten, injuries, Modern Plant Poisoning, infections) and `sickStep` (worse by the kind's rate, a darted animal holds, a mild injury in a fed, watered, clean, calm exhibit mends by `HEALTH.heal`, a hidden case shows at `HEALTH.obviousAt`, a critical warning once, death at 100). `healthNight` only does medicated feed (`e.medFeed`: `feedDoses` a day from whatever the PMC holds, the animals' era medicines first, then contemporary; it cuts infections by `MEDICINE.feedCut` and slows worsening, `medicated`, and heals nothing) and clears the day's trash. `sickName`, `sickCause` and `animalDies` (the condition's name, or "Killed by a ..." when an attack did it) say it in words; treatment: a vet treats a case where it stands below its kind's `field` severity (`fieldTreatable`, `treatHere`: the kind's doses, cured outright; poisonings at any severity), and a check-up (`checkDone`) treats the hidden cases it finds then and there. A worse case is darted (only with a free bed, `bedsFree`; doses or not), stops worsening, and a keeper carries it in. The ward (`state.health.ward`, entries {a, home, cond, med, dosed, done, need, recovered}): `admit` takes the course's `HEALTH.dose` doses up front (`dosePatient`; short, it waits, `outOfMedicine` toasts once a day), and `wardTick` (from `healthTick`) runs a park-minute timer, `wardMinutes` = severity × `HEALTH.wardPerPoint` × `MEDICINE.dockWard[era]` on contemporary medicine or `MEDICINE.eraWard` on the era's own, paused while no vet is on staff; `wardProgress`, `patientStatus`. Done, `recover` sends it home through the delivery system (`sendWardHome`: a `lab` transfer with `ward:true`, so any keeper, custodian or vet carries it; `pickUp` discharges it, which frees the bed; `dropOff` puts it in or crates it; `wardTidy`, from `deliveryTidy`, re-sends one whose trip was lost). Beds: `wardBeds` (`state.health.beds`, from `HEALTH.beds` to `HEALTH.bedsMax`), `buyBed`/`bedPrice` (×`HEALTH.bedGrowth` each), `bedUpkeep` on the PMC's bill. Demolishing the PMC asks first (map.js) and `pmcRemoved` sends every patient home with its severity cut by its progress, wakes darted animals and resets the beds. Vets (`vetsTick`, `vetDecide`) take, in order: escapes, calls, the worst patient (`pickPatient`: poisonings first), overdue check-ups, then deliveries (`vetDeliver`); and which medicine a course uses (`pickMed`: the era's own once researched, `canTreat`, and on hand, else contemporary; there is no per-period refining). The Botany Lab makes the doses in batches (science.js). Genetics: every animal has an Accuracy (`a.q`, the genome % at its cloning, fixed; bought animals 100) and a Genome Quality (`a.gq`, starts the same); `geneFields(a)` (sim.js) is what every copy of an animal carries, with its disorder. `finishClone` rolls a genetic disorder (`rollDisorder`, `DISORDER_ROLL`: up to 60% at Accuracy 60, none at 95 or more) into `a.dis`, one of `DISORDERS` (Osteoporosis, Cardiac Problems, Neurological Disorder, Weakened Immune System, Muscle Fatigue), a trait, not a case: it works from the start through `disFx(a, key)` (illness and injury chances, Fractures in `pickCondition`, worsening in `sickStep`, stress in `stressTarget`, pacing, rest and play in `pickAct`, gait in map.js), a vet's check-up diagnoses it (`a.dx`, `diagnosed`, a toast) and it's shown yellow at the top of the animal's Health list as "Name (Genetic)" and under its row in the exhibit's Health tab until cured. Genome therapy (Research Lab tech `genetherapy`, `GENE`): an exhibit toggle (`e.geneTherapy`, `geneProblem`) a vet visits as its own job after check-ups (`pickGene`, jobs `toGene`/`gene`, `GENE.minutes` per animal, `geneDone`): `geneTherapy(e, who)` treats `geneTargets(e)` (a diagnosed disorder and the lab's genome, `labGenome`, ahead of its Genome Quality, `geneGain`) for `geneCost` each while the money lasts, raising `a.gq` to the lab's genome and curing it; Accuracy never changes. Trash thrown into exhibits: the throwing is in services.js (`throwTick`, called from the guest loop); `e.trash` counts pieces for the day, `trashEaten` is the chance a day an animal eats some and gets Foreign Object Ingestion, and `healthNight` clears it.
- `js/behavior.js`: animal behavior, loaded after themes.js. Each animal carries six needs in `a.need` (`NEED_KEYS`: hunger, thirst, discomfort, lonely, bored, stress; 0 is met, 100 desperate) and an act `a.act` (`ACTS` in data.js: eat, drink, rest, patrol, hunt, forage, social, play, hide, pace) with `a.actFor` minutes and, when pacing, `a.why`. `behaviorTick` (from `tick`, every `BEHAVIOR.step` minutes) runs `needsStep` then `pickAct` per animal off a per-exhibit `behaviorCtx`; `behaviorNight` resets them in `endDay`. Vivarium animals never walk: when their act changes, `vivJump` (map.js) teleports them to the spot that act uses (`actGoal`: dish, water, hide or plant), or back to their home spot (where they first appeared) if the act has none. A goal or home that fails `vivSpot` is pulled toward the middle of the case (`vivPull`); a home with no lamp or pool is drawn from the middle third of the case's depth (`vivMidSpot`, the candidate furthest from the other animals and the fittings), and `quietSpot` in a case is just its middle. Pictures never overlap in a case: `vivBody` is an animal's picture room (half its width, half its body height), `vivCrowd(h, p)` tests a spot against the others and against the bases of tall fittings (an animal is drawn over fittings, so one standing behind a base would hover over it), new homes skip crowded spots and `vivClear` moves a jump's landing spot to the nearest clear one. `place34` draws a case's animals over all its fittings, north to south among themselves. Species traits (`traitsOf`: `active` diurnal/nocturnal/crepuscular via `awake`, `social` solitary/pair/herd/pack via `matesWanted`, `temper` skittish/curious/aggressive) come from `TRAITS` in data.js, else from diet, group and size. Dev-mode gating (`toyHidden`): with `devMode()` off, open-exhibit toy props (`HIDDEN_TOYS`) are hidden from Habitat Props and open exhibits always have full enrichment (`enrichment(e)` is 1); vivarium props (all `vivToy` items, including Misters) are available to everyone, and `enrichment(e)` is real inside vivariums, with `ENRICH.vivBase` (.2) added for non-dev players so a bare vivarium is not harsh on day one; the vivarium panel shows toy points against `enrichNeed` on a meter. Dev mode is unchanged (no baseline, everything counts). Enrichment (`enrichment`, `enrichNeed`, `toyPoints`; `ENRICH`, and the toy props in `LAND`, under Habitat Props' Enrichment sub-tab: `post`/`logs`/`wallow`/`ball` with flat `toy` points, and `icefish`/`icefruit`/`shank`/`buglog`/`hay` with `toyFor` points by diet (`toyFor(t, s)`, `toyPoints(e, s)`, `toyFit` scores each species against `enrichNeedOf`). Items with `lasts` (frozen treats, hay bales) count down `f.left` in `treatsNight` and vanish, frozen ones `ENRICH.melt` faster in a heat wave; hay bales also feed plant eaters (`hay` in `browseRate`), and Paleoflora bales (`pbale`) feed the older ones (`paleo`), taking `ceres` Paleoflora from `state.ceres.stock` in `placeLand` and needing the `paleoflora` tech; vivariums have their own `vivToy` items, `vbark`/`vbranch`/`vdig`/`vfeed`/`vmist`, picked from a dropdown in the vivarium's Exhibit tab, `vivToysHtml`, and set in the glass by `addVivToy`/`vivToyProblem` in landscape.js) slows boredom and lets them play. Midday naps (`BEHAVIOR.nap`, `napLull`, `napRoll`, `a.napFor`/`a.napDay` in `pickAct`): a settled animal awake by day lies down once a day for 30 to 80 minutes, most likely around 13:00 and more in a heat wave, and stays down until the nap is over; `actGoal`'s rest case sends it to the nearest shelter or tree. Attacks (end of behavior.js, `ATTACK` in data.js): `attackStep(e, ctx)` runs each behavior step. A predator (or carnivore, per `attackReason`) with prey in the exhibit gets the `hunt` act (`preyIn`, a pull in `pickAct`) only while `huntDriven` (hunger, stress or boredom over the `ATTACK` lines; a fed, calm one ignores prey). Buying, cloning, placing or moving an animal into an exhibit where someone hunts someone asks first (`mixWarning`, a confirm dialog in ui.js), map.js `actGoal` walks it up to the nearest prey, and once it has stalked `ATTACK.stalk` minutes and is within `ATTACK.strike` it bites; stressed aggressive (or crowded) animals fight. `looseAttacks` (from `escapesTick`) lets a loose predator within `ATTACK.reach` of an exhibit it can get into (`canGetIn`: breached, or stronger than the fence) attack. `hurtAnimal(e, v, by, how, range, killP, msg, who)` adds an injury (`a.sick.hit` {by, how, who: the attacker's name, for "From fight with VELO-005."}, which `animalDies` turns into the death-dialog cause), fires the dust fx, and `a.hitAt`/`l.biteAt` space attacks by `ATTACK.gap`. Effects: `behaviorScore` in `exhibitReport` (stress, boredom, the Pacing/Hiding tells), `liveliness` on appeal in `recompute`, `stressEscape` in `escapesTick`, `BEHAVIOR.stressIll` in `illChance`, and the `pacing`/`playful` guest thoughts in `seeExhibit`. map.js walks each act (`animalPlan`, `actGoal`, `paceRoute`, `patrolRoute`, `ACT_GAIT`): pacing animals get an amber dashed ring and hiding ones fade. The exhibit panel's Behavior tab is `behaviorHtml` in ui.js. Going inside (behavior.js "going inside", `SHELTER` in data.js): `shelterStep` runs after `pickAct` each step and sends an animal to the nearest shelter (`LAND` item with `slots`, via `shelterFits`/`shelterTake`, capacity tracked per step in `ctx.load`) to hide (act `hide`), sleep (resting while `awake` is at or under `SHELTER.sleepBelow`, or on a nap; the park being shut counts as night for day animals in `awake`) or wait out bad weather (storm, heat wave, cold snap, a share of animals per day, `shelterWhy`). `shelterRun` (animal id to {f, why, in, since, take}) is runtime only; `a.inside` ("hide"/"sleep"/"weather") is set once it arrives (`shelterArrive`, also called per frame by `animateAnimals`), cleared when the reason ends, every night and by `upgradeSave`. map.js: `actGoal` walks it to its shelter, `animateAnimals` hides the element while `a.inside` and skips it, `animalAt`/`drawAnimalRing` ignore it, `actLabel` says "Sheltering from the storm"/"Sleeping inside"/"Hiding inside". If every shelter is full it stays outside. Guests: `visibleShare(e)` (hiding counts fully, sleeping inside by day half and never at night, weather a quarter, `HIDING.weights`) is averaged in `hideAvg` over `HIDING.window`; `hideBother(p, e)` (only past `HIDING.from`, divided by `pm(p, "hideTol")`, cut by `p.edu`) trims mood and the visit's pleasure in `seeExhibit` and adds the "hiding" thought, and `behaviorScore`/`behaviorHtml` show the hint. Privacy Screens (`LAND.screen`, `SCREEN`, `screenShare`, drawn by `screen34`) and Quiet Signs (`BUILDINGS.quiet`, `QUIET`, `quietSigns`, `guestsSeen`) both cut `ctx.seen`, the crowd skittish animals feel. Land animals keep out of exhibit water (`walkArea`'s `wat`); one found standing in a pond (water drawn over it, or a spawn with no dry spot) is walked out by `wadeOut` (map.js) at its next plan.
- Animal sex: every animal has `a.sex` ("male"/"female"; `SEX` in data.js; `pickSex`, `sexMark`, `sexSplit` in sim.js; `nameAnimal` assigns one if missing (and `a.face`, 1 right or -1 left, random: the way its picture first faces, which is what a vivarium animal keeps), `upgradeSave` migrates, and copies of animals must carry `sex` like `name`). Genetics Lab clone jobs carry it (`orderClone(sp, exhibitId, sex)`; Clone M / Clone F buttons). Effects: `singleSexKinds` (behavior.js) makes pair/herd species with one sex lonelier (`SEX.lonelyMult`) and less happy (`SEX.happy`); `fightBias(a)` is the male-aggression hook for animal attacks. No breeding yet. Social system (behavior.js "social life", `SOCIAL` in data.js; open exhibits only, vivariums ignore it): `a.rank` (saved, 0 alpha to 1 bottom; `pickRank`, `byRank`, `rankPos`, shown in the animal panel) orders herds and packs. `socialInfo(e)` reads each species' `traitsOf(s).social` and the sexes: herd/pack all-male or all-female are stable and happy, one male with females is stable, two or more males fight (`rivalFight` from `attackStep`, via `hurtAnimal`, so injuries and dust; loser stressed, lowest rank worst, scaled by males and room), loners are stressed by company (same sex worst, eased by room, rocks and shelter), pairs want one male and one female. `socialStress` feeds `stressTarget`, `behaviorScore` adds the happiness and a "Social." line, and the Behavior tab lists it. Packs (`traitsOf(s).social === "pack"`, `PACK` in data.js) skip the herd rules: `packOrder` puts the alpha pair (best rank plus the best of the other sex) first and `socialInfo` returns a `pack` entry (too small, no alpha pair, short of food; also for one animal alone); `eatTick` (keepers.js) fills `packFeed` so shortages hit subordinates first (`needsStep` hunger); mates join a hunt in `pickAct` and `packHuntBoost` raises the strike chance; `packChallenge` (from `attackStep`) is a rare, stress-driven rank swap. Vivariums ignore all of it.
- `js/animalui.js`: individual animals. Every animal has `a.name` (default species id uppercased + 3-digit counter, e.g. `COTY-001`; `nameAnimal(a)` in sim.js, counter `state.animalSeq` so numbers are never reused; `upgradeSave` names old animals; every place that copies an animal object (escapes, holding, ward, cloning) must carry `name`). Tapping an animal in select mode (`animalAt`) opens `sel = {kind:"animal"}`: `animalHtml`/`animalLive` (editable name, max 24 chars, mood, needs, Health, wants, stressors). The Health section opens with a 0 to 100% health bar, `animalHp(a)` (medicine.js: 100 minus the sickness's severity, full while it's well or its illness is still hidden; `hpColor` picks green, yellow or red), the same bar each animal gets in the exhibit's Animals tab (`animalsTabHtml`: every animal by name with its own Move (the move dialog for just that one, `requestMove`'s `id`; Moving while queued) and Sell, then waiting moves; the Exhibit tab's "Animals here" only lists counts) and in the Health tab's list of every animal, worst first. Below it is `animalHealthItems(a, e, s)`: condition and severity (injury cause from `a.sick.hit`/`cause`, `sickStatus`; hidden illness never shows), a diagnosed genetic disorder on top, Accuracy and Genome Quality, medicated today, the exhibit's last vet check-up. `basicsLow(e, list)` (medicine.js) adds low food stock, hungry, no water, thirsty and low sanitation lines (only when low) to both that section and the exhibit's Health tab, ringed by `drawAnimalRing`.
- Land animals and water (map.js): only `semi` species (and flyers) may enter exhibit water. `walkArea` gives everyone else `wat` plus `wm` (half a body), so `inWater`/`walkClear` treat a pond as its polygon grown by `wm`; `wadeOut` hops a stranded animal to the bank rather than routing across; the step loop in `animateAnimals` refuses any step that goes into water (`landWet`).
- Crates (science.js, `CRATE` in data.js), Zoo Tycoon style: an animal boxed off the map turns into a crate standing in the park, `state.crates` (`crates()`): the animal's record (name, sex, rank, Accuracy, Genome Quality, disorder, clone flag, illness) plus `x`, `y`. There is no holding list or fee. A crate is in no exhibit, so nothing feeds, ages or treats it and guests never see it, until `uncrate(id, e)` opens it into an exhibit (`crateProblem(r, e)` says why it can't in a few words, `fitsHabitat`; a pool animal also wants its water in, `putAnimal` brings it back whole). `crateAnimal(e, a)` is instant and leaves the crate where the animal stood (`bodyPos`); the Crate button on the animal panel and the Animals tab (`crateAnimalNow` in animalui.js), pool animals whose exhibit loses its lagoon wall or all its water (`crateStranded`, run first in `afterChange`) and the pool animals of a bulldozed exhibit or gate (`removeItem` > `releaseAnimals` in map.js; every other animal there gets out through `turnLoose` in escapes.js, with no rating hit) all use it. A recaptured escapee where it was caught, and a moved or discharged animal with nowhere to go at its old exhibit or the PMC (`dropCrate(a, x, y)`, `labSpot`). Finished clones are not crates, see Specimens below. A crate stands on free ground only (`crateSpotProblem`: inside the park, not on a building, path, fence, case, water, another crate or within `CRATE.entrance` m of the entrance; inside an open exhibit is fine), and `crateSpotNear` finds the nearest free spot in rings. `moveCrate(id, x, y)` sets it down elsewhere and `sellCrate(id)` sells it at `COST.animalResale`. A crate is the picture `sprites/crate.png` (`tools/pixelcrate.py`) scaled to the animal (`crateScale`: its `animalRadius` over `CRATE.perRadius`, from `CRATE.min` to `CRATE.max`). Old saves' `science.ready` become crates with no spot (`x` null) and `settleCrates` puts them by the lab. The UI is in docs/ui.md.
- Specimens and deliveries (science.js, keepers.js): a finished clone joins `state.science.specimens` (`specimens()`), the animal's record, and waits at the Genetics Lab; the lab panel's Specimens tab (`specimensHtml`, scienceui.js) lists them with where each is headed, who has it, and Move (`startDeliver` in map.js: the crate rides the pointer and a tap on an exhibit it fits, green or red with the reason, calls `sendSpecimen` in ui.js) or Cancel (`cancelDelivery`, before pickup). A clone ordered for an exhibit queues its delivery on completion (`finishClone`). `deliverSpecimen(id, exhibitId)` checks `crateProblem`, a connected lab (the `tar` stop on the staff graph) and a keeper gate, then adds a transfer `{lab:true, from:lab id, to:exhibit id}` to `state.staff.transfers` and hands it to the nearest idle keeper, custodian or vet (`assignDelivery`); if nobody is idle, the first to look for work takes it (`deliveryJob`, in `decide`, `custDecide`, `vetDecide`, after hunts and calls, before chores, patients and check-ups). The worker walks to the lab (`toPickup`), carries the crate (`c.crate`, drawn small in the hands by `carryCrate` in map.js) and drops it at the gate (`toDropoff`); `dropOff` puts the animal in with `putAnimal`, or back on the list if the exhibit can't take it. The specimen stays listed until it's in. Keepers use `arrive`; vets and custodians use `labPickup`/`labDrop`. Moving an animal between exhibits works the same way: the animal's Move button (`startAnimalMove`, map.js, crate mode `move`) lets you tap the destination exhibit, `sendMove` (ui.js) asks the predator and flyer questions and calls `requestMove` (keepers.js), which queues a transfer `{any:true, from:exhibit, to:exhibit}` and hands it to the nearest idle worker; `deliveryJob` and `moveJob` treat `any` like `lab`, and `pickUp` takes the animal out of its exhibit. At the 4 AM rollover a carried specimen is delivered at once (`keepersNight`), a firing finishes it off (`fireStaff`), and a delivery whose exhibit or lab is gone is dropped (`deliveryTidy`, in `afterChange`).
