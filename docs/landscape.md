# Landscape, biomes, themes, ground paint

Moved out of CLAUDE.md. Update this file (not CLAUDE.md) when the code it describes changes.

- **Biome names:** the keys `temperate` and `boreal` (sprites, patterns, plant tables, saves) are shown to players as "Seasonal Forest" and "Coniferous Forest" (`BIOMES[k].label`). Don't rename the keys.
- **Animal biomes:** each species has two biomes in `SPECIES_BIOMES` (data.js), equally at home in either, and the order means nothing. `biomeFit` is "home" (either biome, `BIOME_HAPPY.home`) or "away" (`BIOME_HAPPY.away`). The one place both matter is `waterWant`: an animal is a dry-ground one only when both its biomes are scrubland or desert.
- **Fern Prairie** (`fern`): the open ground of the eras before grass, with plants Permian through Paleogene only (grassland is Neogene on). Its art is still scrubland's copied under the `fern` names (see tools/BIOME_ART.md), and the Permian plant names are still placeholders (Triassic through Paleogene have real names).

- `js/landscape.js`: water, rocks, groves and shelters inside open exhibits. Water is laid as chunk boxes (`e.water`, `WATER`, `waterProblem`/`waterHost`/`addWater`; the `water` tool in map.js drags a box in whole 5 m chunks like paths: `wdrag`, `waterBox`, `endWaterDrag`; each box is a 4-corner rectangle, `rectOfPts`/`waterHit` treat edge-touching rectangles as not overlapping, and `mergeWater` joins a new one to a neighbour of the same width or height; water is not reshaped or moved by hand: `reshapeables` (the Move tool's corners) lists only exhibit fences, and Move shifts an exhibit that holds water in whole 5 m steps (`moveStep`), so water stays on the chunk grid. In an exhibit a chunk is cut to the fence with `waterClip(host, box)` (half-meter cells wholly inside the fence with `WATER.margin` = 0.5 m to spare, joined into rectangles: a straight wall gives one box, a slanted one steps; pieces under `WATER.minArea` are dropped; no host, the park, keeps the box); the `water` tool lays each part, and the ghost shows them. `insideFence` refuses a shore closer than the margin, and `reshapeProblem` uses it, so a fence can't be pulled onto water. Exhibit water is drawn in map.js right after the floor and before the fence (`landSvg` no longer draws it), so posts and rails stand over the bank. `state.waterGrid5` marks saves already through `snapWaterToGrid` (called from `upgradeSave`): older water (corner-drawn, or moved in 1 m steps) becomes whole chunks, each kept when the body covers at least half of what its host leaves of the chunk, then cut by `waterClip`, with no money changing hands; water has no bulldoze pick, `watersSvg` draws it with no hit shape and `bulldozeTap` ignores it: ground paint over it takes it away, see the paint paragraph. `cutWater(host, rect)` cuts a box out of one host's water (`null` is the park's `state.water`) and returns `{m2, left}`, with the same piece maths `layWater` uses to make room for a new box; `cutWaterAll(rect)` cuts every host, joins the leftovers with `mergeWater` and pays `waterCutRefund(m2)` (build price at `COST.refundShare`); `waterCutM2(rect)` is the dry run for the cost line), and old saves' round ponds become drawn water in `upgradeSave`. The rest are placed with a tap (`e.land`, `LAND`, `HAB`, `likesOf` in data.js). `habitatOf`/`habitatScore` feed `exhibitReport`, and each species wants its own water share (`waterWant` in data.js: 25% semi-aquatic fish eaters, 5% for a scrubland or desert home biome, 8% for the rest), rocks and plants (`wantsOf` vs `haveOf`, `speciesFit`; `likesOf`, `BIOMES[x].rock`/`plants`, `HAB.waterMax`; too much water counts against it too), `thirsty` feeds `illChance`. Groves (`LAND[x].flora`) from an animal's era add happiness, `browseShare` cuts what `eatTick` drains, and `grassShare(e, s)` (0 to 1: Cenozoic plants as a share of the exhibit's plants, a grassland biome, or grass hay in place of Paleoflora) is how much of an old grazer's food is grass it never evolved for: it feeds `grassSick` (illness, `GRASS_INTOLERANT`) and the happiness hit in `exhibitReport`. Landscape plants are generated from `PLANT_TABLE` in data.js: one small, medium and large per period and biome (keys like `jur-tropical-small`, `PLANTS_OF[period][biome]`), so a biome that didn't exist in a period has none (no grassland before the Neogene). They carry `period` and `biome` and `landProblem` only lets them go in an exhibit of that biome. An animal counts plants from its own period in full toward what it wants, and other periods' plants of the exhibit's biome up to `HAB.offEra` (a third) of what it wants (`haveOf(e, s)`, `plantSuits`), and wants none where its period had no plants in the exhibit's biome (`wantsOf`). The build menu has a dropdown per period and the exhibit panel lists the biome's plants by period. Each Mesozoic or Paleozoic plant uses one plant of its period and size (`LAND[x].size`) from `state.ceres.pots`, which CERES grows in batches (`CERES_GROW.plant`). Biomes (`BIOMES`, `SPECIES_BIOMES`/`biomesOf` in data.js): each open exhibit has `e.biome` (`biomeOf` falls back to `DEFAULT_BIOME`), and each open-exhibit species has a home biome and one it gets by in. `biomeFit` feeds `habitatScore` (`BIOME_HAPPY`), wetland adds to `wet` in `habitatOf`. map.js fills open exhibits with the biome color and its `#b-<biome>` pattern from index.html (vivariums keep the period tint; animal dots stay period colors). Weather (`state.weather` {today, next, from}, `WEATHER` in data.js) is rolled each night by `rollWeather` in `endDay`, after the night functions so today's weather counts for tonight's illness. `exposure(e)`, the share of the herd without cover from shelters (`LAND[x].slots`) or grove shade, feeds happiness, `illChance`, `injuryChance` (storms) and `wearPerDay`, and `weatherNow().guests` scales demand. `landSpot`/`placeLand`/`landSvg` do placement and drawing. The tools (`land-rock`, `land-cycads`, ...) are handled in map.js (`landHover`/`landTap`), and water and land features move, reshape-check and bulldoze with their exhibit (water on its own, no). Vivarium scenes: a vivarium takes no plants; it has a scene, `e.vivScene = {era, biome}` (null for none; an optional `period` picks a period scene once one is listed in `VIV_SCENE_PERIODS`), set in the panel (`vivSceneHtml` in ui.js: an Era select and a Biome select over all of `BIOMES`, action `vivScene`; the scene's biome is also the case's biome for `biomeOf`) by `setVivScene`/`vivSceneProblem` for the flat `VIV_SCENE.price` (clearing it refunds `COST.refundShare`). `sceneFit(e, s)` is half for the right era (or period) and half for one of the species' biomes; `haveOf` gives a vivarium `sceneFit` times what the species wants in plants, so `speciesFit`, the `VIV_PLANT.bonus` in `habitatScore` and the sim.js own-era check run off the scene. Old saves turn a case's plants into the scene of its `e.biome` and its animals' era (`upgradeSave`, `s.vivScene`). The pictures are drawn by `vivScene34` in map.js (art.md). Vivarium fittings (vivarium animals only; `LAND` items with `vivToy`, `addVivToy`/`vivToyProblem`, panel section `vivToysHtml` in ui.js: Fittings and Enrichment for everyone): the food dish (see keepers.js), a Basking Lamp (`lamp`, `VIV_LAMP`; `wantsHeat(s)` is every pre-Cenozoic species not in `WARM_BLOODED`; one lamp warms `per` animals, `heatShare(e)`: happiness up and down in `habitatScore`, comfort in `comfortOf`, illness down and a cold snap hurts the unlit in `illChance`, upkeep via `LAND.upkeep` in `dailyCosts`) and a Pool (`pool`, `poolM2` of water, `VIV_POOL`: counts as water in `haveOf`, ends `thirsty` for water lovers, which a vivarium now is without one, and `behaviorCtx` water). Fittings spread over the whole floor: `vivPlantSpot(e, key, skip, k)` puts the first at the middle of the case (`VIV_PLANT.shift` toward the front) and each next at the spot with the most room to the others (up to `VIV_PLANT.apart`), keeping the sill margin (`VIV34.sill / TILT + .15`) so nothing hides behind the front frame; `relayViv(e)` lays a case's fittings out again that way, run once per save by `upgradeSave` (`state.vivLay`). Animals gather: `vivHomes(e, s)` (lamp glow for heat lovers, pool for water lovers) feed `vivHomeSpot` in map.js `syncAnimals`, `regatherViv` re-places them when a fitting comes or goes, and one standing in a pool (`vivPoolAt`, `h.sub`) is drawn cut at the waterline with a ripple (in a case the cut sits lower for low-slung pictures like the Tiktaalik, so their body still shows; the ripple stays at the waterline). Drawn by `vivPropSvg`/`lampLightSvg` from `sprites/viv/<key>.png` (`VIV_SPRITES` in data.js: pixel size, `flat` for the pool, dish and light, which sort under the animals at `data-y` minus 1000; made by `tools/pixelviv.py`, the five old props and the new ones in the flat style).
  Water tiles: a biome in `WATER_TILES` (data.js) has its `#w-<biome>` pattern rebuilt by `buildGroundTiles` (biomepaint.js, the ground tile builder with its own salt) from 1 m tiles `sprites/water/<biome>-<n>.png`: `plain` tiles are one solid color, `detail` tiles carry a mark, `share` of each 5 m chunk's cells take a detail tile, `turn: false` mirrors but never turns. `glint` extra tiles are an animation: the first `glint` of `frames` frames on every plain cell (each cell at its own random start, or with `waves` (a list of [period in frames, wavelength in cells]; wavelengths must divide the 100 m pattern) by its x + y, so bands of glints sweep down and right, and waves of different periods drift in and out of step (the loop is their least common multiple, `frames` is ignored), `fps` frames a second, the pattern's image swapped by one interval), the other frames the plain tile (glints are never mirrored or turned, so they always travel one way); wetland: 3 glint tiles (`wetland-3..5`), waves [12 frames, 10 cells] and [24 frames, 25 cells] (a 24 frame loop), 4 fps. Wetland is the first (one solid tile, one clump tile with 1 or 2 a chunk, three glint tiles); Fern Prairie uses the same recipe with two glint tiles (`fern-1..4`, same waves) and a 1 px shore line (`WATER_TEX.fern.shore` `#988B4A`, `shoreW` 1, no bank; the colour is from the artist's N/S/E/W edge tiles, which aren't loaded as files); Seasonal Forest (`temperate`) uses it too (`temperate-1` plain, `temperate-2..4` glints, no detail tile, same waves, shore `#5C4A3D` from the artist's edge tile, 1 px, no bank); Scrubland uses it too (`scrubland-1` plain, `scrubland-2..4` glints, no detail tile, same waves, shore `#B59A6D`, 1 px, no bank); the older biomes still use a 144 px `sprites/water/<biome>.png` from `tools/pixelwater_flat.py`. The shore: a `WATER_TEX` biome with `shore` (wetland: `#827B24`, the 1 px north/south/east/west edge tiles) has `waterMask` draw that color on every edge that touches land, in place of the lit and dark rims, and a biome without `damp`/`mud` has no bank around the water. `softenShore` (landscape.js, called from `waterMask`) makes that shore less square for biomes with `shore`: pixels next to land are eroded 0, 1 or 2 px by smooth noise of the world position (`SHORE_NOISE`, `SHORE_ERODE`), then convex corners are chipped `SHORE_CHIP` times. The shore itself is a band `shoreW` px wide (wetland 2), measured inward from the land after the softening, so it follows the stair-steps and corners. It removes only that kind's pixels that touch land, never water of another kind, so joins stay closed, and the same shape always gives the same edge. It is opt-in (only a biome with `shore`), drawing only, and capped at 2 px of erosion so it stays inside the 0.5 m fence margin; its constants are shared by every biome.
  Moving land: the Move tool picks up any rock, plant, shelter or statue (`moveTarget` takes kind `land`; the Move tool turns on the bulldoze hit shapes, `pick`, for land). It drags with `footSnap` and the item rides along live while `footGhostSvg` shows the footprint (a rock's rects) it will take (red when it won't fit). `landMoveSpot(m, wx, wy)` finds the target (the open exhibit under the spot, else the park; a vivarium fitting stays in its own case and nothing else goes in one) and checks it with `landFit` (exhibit) / `decorFit` (park) / the glass rule for a case, each with the item itself skipped. `landProblem` and `decorProblem` are those fit checks plus the research, CERES, pot and price checks, which a move skips: moving is free and keeps the item's id. `dropLand` (map.js) splices it between `e.land` and `state.decor`, pins a rock's `biome` to the one it had so it keeps its stone, resets the walking animals' paths in both exhibits, calls `regatherViv` for a vivarium and `afterChange`. Shelters in another exhibit are dropped by `shelterStep` (behavior.js).
  Park landscaping (outside exhibits) lives here too. `landSpot` falls back to `decorProblem` when the point isn't in an open exhibit, and `placeLand(null, ...)` puts the item in `state.decor`. Water drawn outside exhibits goes in `state.water` (`waterProblem(pts, null)` → `parkWaterProblem`, `addWater(null, pts)`), and old open fence lines in `state.fences` (`fenceProblem`, `addFenceLine`, `fenceLineCost` in sim.js; kind `"fence"` in map.js for select/move/bulldoze; nothing builds new ones now, the grid fence in fences.js does). `parkFences()` is both those and the loose grid fence, and is what paths, buildings, water, decor and the guests' hedge cheer check. New paths, buildings and exhibits must keep clear of all of it: `shapeHitsLandscape(pts)` and `lineHitsLandscape(pts, hw, bridge)`; only a path with `type:"bridge"` (`isBridge`, `BRIDGE` in data.js) crosses park water. Named modern plants (`PARK_PLANTS` → `LAND["q-<name>"]`, `PARK_PLANTS_OF[biome]`) are Quaternary plants usable anywhere, with `look`/`accent` for drawing (`plantSvg`) and `wet`/`aquatic` for water. Other periods' plants need the ORACLE tech `sterile` to go outside exhibits. Statues (`STATUES` → `LAND["st-<id>"]`, `statue` field) are dev-mode only for v1.0 (`statuesOn()` in data.js = `devMode()`: with it off the Statues dropdown is hidden, `statueOpen` is false so `decorProblem` refuses them, and `grantStatue(g)` is null so grants and the award toast never mention or unlock one; statues already in a save keep loading, drawing and cheering guests) and go outside exhibits only; a few are `free`, the rest unlock with a grant's `statue` field (`statueOpen`, `statueGrant`, shown by `grantPrize`). A statue's plinth uses its theme's path color (`statueSvg`). Guests: `indexDecor(nodes)` (called from `buildGuestGraph`) marks each guest-path node with `n.decor` (0 to 1, `DECOR`) and `n.statues`; `guestsTick` turns decor into mood and the "pretty" thought, and `statueSeen` gives learning (people's statues teach far more). Drawing: `waterSvg`, `featSvg`, `parkWaterSvg`, `decorSvg`, `fenceLineSvg`, `hedgeSvg` (hedge rows, `BARRIERS.hedge`, are drawn as real hedges in exhibits too).
- `js/themes.js`: themes (`THEMES`, `THEME` in data.js).  Zones can carry a `theme` (`zoneThemePlan`, `restyleZone`, `themedZoneAt`): it tints the map under the zone (`THEMES[x].ground`), new builds inside use it, and a panel button restyles what is already there (UI in logiui.js `zoneThemeHtml`). Genesis (the default, no `theme` field) is a full theme too: a conservation campus of forest green, matte gold and warm concrete. `roofs` sort buildings by job (green standing seam for public ones, pale flat lab roofs with `units` for ORACLE/GHOST/TAR/CERES/PMC, dark slate for utilities), exhibits get the steel rail or panel wall with amber post lights while an electric fence is powered (map.js). Paths, buildings and exhibits carry an optional `theme` (none means Genesis) that recolors them (`themeOf`, `themeFill`). `state.themes` holds the build `brush` and the unlocked `have` list; `checkThemes` (called from `checkGoals`) unlocks them by play (Gilded Age by the `gilded` ORACLE tech, Bayou by keeping a water-loving animal, Volcanic by a 4-star rating). `themeZone(e)` checks that the guest paths and shops and restrooms within `THEME.radius` of an exhibit all match it (non-Genesis only), which adds appeal in `recompute`, and `themeFitShare` (species in `THEMES[x].fits`) lifts happiness in `exhibitReport`. Changing a theme costs `fee` of the item's price (`themeFee`, `themeProblem`); new builds use the brush (`themeNew`). The pickers are in ui.js (`themeHtml`, `themesOverview`). Loads after landscape.js.
- `js/biomepaint.js`: paintable biome ground outside exhibits, loaded just before map.js. **Ground tiles:** a biome listed in `GROUND_TILES` (data.js: `{plain, detail, share}`) has 8 x 8 px, 1 m tiles, `sprites/ground/<biome>-<n>.png` (8 px a meter, so 0.125 m a pixel, like the 18 m `<biome>.png` tiles): the first `plain` are plain ground, the next `detail` carry a mark. `buildGroundTiles` (end of biomepaint.js) composes a 100 m square with one tile per 1 m cell: in each 5 m chunk about `share` of the cells (the lowest hashes, `groundPicks`) get a detail tile, the rest plain, and each cell is turned and mirrored at random by `cellHash` (except tiles in `still`, like boreal's lit stone; all three wetland, scrubland, desert, and temperate and tropical tiles are still, and tundra's first two; grassland's second), so it is fixed between draws and reloads. The result goes into both the `#p-<biome>` (park ground, paint outside exhibits) and `#b-<biome>` (exhibit floors) patterns' images. To add or replace tiles, see `tools/BIOME_ART.md` section 1b. A tiled biome's park ground and paint skip the dark-mode dimming layer (`groundShade`, biomepaint.js), so they match the exhibit floors, which are never dimmed. `state.biomePaint` is a list of `{id, biome, r:[x0,y0,x1,y1]}` patches on the 1 m grid, drawn in list order by `paintSvg` (cached by `paintVer`; `#p-<biome>` pattern over the biome's `park` color, clipped to `#plotClip`) in `render` just above the park ground and under paths, buildings and exhibits. It is decoration only; `state.biome` stays locked. `paintRect(biome, box)` deletes patches the new one fully covers, trims partly covered ones when one rectangle is left, merges same-biome neighbours when nothing above them overlaps, and ignores a repaint of what is already on top; `eraseRect` splits patches, `erasePaintAt`, `repickPaint(id, biome)` (tap a patch with the tool). The `paint` tool (Landscaping > Paint Ground; `paintBar`, `endPaintDrag`, `pdrag`, `paintPick` biome or "erase" in map.js, biome buttons in `#dSizes`) drags a box in whole 5 m chunks like the path tools (`pdrag` holds chunk coords, `paintBox(a, b)` turns two chunks into meters, `paintBoxW` does it from world points; a tap paints or erases one chunk; old patches keep their odd sizes); Bulldoze on bare ground also takes paint off (`bulldozePaint`). `upgradeSave` defaults the list. Inside open exhibits (not vivariums) the same paint is drawn over the floor (`exhibitPaintSvg`, clipped to the exhibit) and decides the exhibit's biome: `biomeOf(e)` (landscape.js) is `paintedBiome(e)`, the biome with the most sampled ground (`paintShare`, cached per exhibit), where unpainted ground counts as `e.biome` and a tie keeps it. Painting inside exhibits costs `exhibitPaintCost` (the biome's `perSqM` for each m² not already that biome); out in the park it's free. The exhibit panel's Biome section (`biomeHtml`) shows the split and a Paint ground button. A vivarium has no Biome section: its biome is its scene's (`biomeOf` reads `e.vivScene.biome`, else the `e.biome` it was built with), set from the Scene panel.
