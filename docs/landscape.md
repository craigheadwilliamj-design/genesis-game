# Landscape, biomes, themes, ground paint

Moved out of CLAUDE.md. Update this file (not CLAUDE.md) when the code it describes changes.

- `js/landscape.js`: water, rocks, groves and shelters inside open exhibits. Water is laid as chunk boxes (`e.water`, `WATER`, `waterProblem`/`waterHost`/`addWater`; the `water` tool in map.js drags a box in whole 5 m chunks like paths: `wdrag`, `waterBox`, `endWaterDrag`; each box is a 4-corner rectangle, `rectOfPts`/`waterHit` treat edge-touching rectangles as not overlapping, and `mergeWater` joins a new one to a neighbour of the same width or height; water is not reshaped or moved by hand: `reshapeables` (the Move tool's corners) lists only exhibit fences, and Move shifts an exhibit that holds water in whole 5 m steps (`moveStep`), so water stays on the chunk grid. In an exhibit a chunk is cut to the fence with `waterClip(host, box)` (half-meter cells wholly inside the fence with `WATER.margin` = 0.5 m to spare, joined into rectangles: a straight wall gives one box, a slanted one steps; pieces under `WATER.minArea` are dropped; no host, the park, keeps the box); the `water` tool lays each part, and the ghost shows them. `insideFence` refuses a shore closer than the margin, and `reshapeProblem` uses it, so a fence can't be pulled onto water. Exhibit water is drawn in map.js right after the floor and before the fence (`landSvg` no longer draws it), so posts and rails stand over the bank. `state.waterGrid5` marks saves already through `snapWaterToGrid` (called from `upgradeSave`): older water (corner-drawn, or moved in 1 m steps) becomes whole chunks, each kept when the body covers at least half of what its host leaves of the chunk, then cut by `waterClip`, with no money changing hands; water has no bulldoze pick, `watersSvg` draws it with no hit shape and `bulldozeTap` ignores it: ground paint over it takes it away, see the paint paragraph. `cutWater(host, rect)` cuts a box out of one host's water (`null` is the park's `state.water`) and returns `{m2, left}`, with the same piece maths `layWater` uses to make room for a new box; `cutWaterAll(rect)` cuts every host, joins the leftovers with `mergeWater` and pays `waterCutRefund(m2)` (build price at `COST.refundShare`); `waterCutM2(rect)` is the dry run for the cost line), and old saves' round ponds become drawn water in `upgradeSave`. The rest are placed with a tap (`e.land`, `LAND`, `HAB`, `likesOf` in data.js). `habitatOf`/`habitatScore` feed `exhibitReport`, and each species wants its own water share, rocks and plants (`wantsOf` vs `haveOf`, `speciesFit`; `likesOf`, `BIOMES[x].rock`/`plants`, `HAB.waterMax`; too much water counts against it too), `thirsty` feeds `illChance`. Groves (`LAND[x].flora`) from an animal's era add happiness, `browseShare` cuts what `eatTick` drains, and `grassShare(e, s)` (0 to 1: Cenozoic plants as a share of the exhibit's plants, a grassland biome, or grass hay in place of Paleoflora) is how much of an old grazer's food is grass it never evolved for: it feeds `grassSick` (illness, `GRASS_INTOLERANT`) and the happiness hit in `exhibitReport`. Landscape plants are generated from `PLANT_TABLE` in data.js: one small, medium and large per period and biome (keys like `jur-tropical-small`, `PLANTS_OF[period][biome]`), so a biome that didn't exist in a period has none (no grassland before the Neogene). They carry `period` and `biome` and `landProblem` only lets them go in an exhibit of that biome. An animal counts plants from its own period in full toward what it wants, and other periods' plants of the exhibit's biome up to `HAB.offEra` (a third) of what it wants (`haveOf(e, s)`, `plantSuits`), and wants none where its period had no plants in the exhibit's biome (`wantsOf`). The build menu has a dropdown per period and the exhibit panel lists the biome's plants by period. Each Mesozoic or Paleozoic plant uses one plant of its period and size (`LAND[x].size`) from `state.ceres.pots`, which CERES grows in batches (`CERES_GROW.plant`). Biomes (`BIOMES`, `SPECIES_BIOMES`/`biomesOf` in data.js): each open exhibit has `e.biome` (`biomeOf` falls back to `DEFAULT_BIOME`), and each open-exhibit species has a home biome and one it gets by in. `biomeFit` feeds `habitatScore` (`BIOME_HAPPY`), wetland adds to `wet` in `habitatOf`, and `regradeProblem`/`regradeCost` back the panel's Biome picker. map.js fills open exhibits with the biome color and its `#b-<biome>` pattern from index.html (vivariums keep the period tint; animal dots stay period colors). Weather (`state.weather` {today, next, from}, `WEATHER` in data.js) is rolled each night by `rollWeather` in `endDay`, after the night functions so today's weather counts for tonight's illness. `exposure(e)`, the share of the herd without cover from shelters (`LAND[x].slots`) or grove shade, feeds happiness, `illChance`, `injuryChance` (storms) and `wearPerDay`, and `weatherNow().guests` scales demand. `landSpot`/`placeLand`/`landSvg` do placement and drawing. The tools (`land-rock`, `land-cycads`, ...) are handled in map.js (`landHover`/`landTap`), and water and land features move, reshape-check and bulldoze with their exhibit (water on its own, no). Vivarium fittings (vivarium animals only; `LAND` items with `vivToy`, `addVivToy`/`vivToyProblem`, panel section `vivToysHtml` in ui.js: Fittings and Enrichment for everyone): the food dish (see keepers.js), a Basking Lamp (`lamp`, `VIV_LAMP`; `wantsHeat(s)` is every pre-Cenozoic species not in `WARM_BLOODED`; one lamp warms `per` animals, `heatShare(e)`: happiness up and down in `habitatScore`, comfort in `comfortOf`, illness down and a cold snap hurts the unlit in `illChance`, upkeep via `LAND.upkeep` in `dailyCosts`) and a Pool (`pool`, `poolM2` of water, `VIV_POOL`: counts as water in `haveOf`, ends `thirsty` for water lovers, which a vivarium now is without one, and `behaviorCtx` water). Animals gather: `vivHomes(e, s)` (lamp glow for heat lovers, pool for water lovers) feed `vivHomeSpot` in map.js `syncAnimals`, `regatherViv` re-places them when a fitting comes or goes, and one standing in a pool (`vivPoolAt`, `h.sub`) is drawn cut at the waterline with a ripple. Drawn by `vivPropSvg`/`lampLightSvg` from `sprites/viv/<key>.png` (`VIV_SPRITES` in data.js: pixel size, `flat` for the pool, dish and light, which sort under the animals at `data-y` minus 1000; made by `tools/pixelviv.py`, the five old props and the new ones in the flat style).
  Park landscaping (outside exhibits) lives here too. `landSpot` falls back to `decorProblem` when the point isn't in an open exhibit, and `placeLand(null, ...)` puts the item in `state.decor`. Water drawn outside exhibits goes in `state.water` (`waterProblem(pts, null)` → `parkWaterProblem`, `addWater(null, pts)`), and old open fence lines in `state.fences` (`fenceProblem`, `addFenceLine`, `fenceLineCost` in sim.js; kind `"fence"` in map.js for select/move/bulldoze; nothing builds new ones now, the grid fence in fences.js does). `parkFences()` is both those and the loose grid fence, and is what paths, buildings, water, decor and the guests' hedge cheer check. New paths, buildings and exhibits must keep clear of all of it: `shapeHitsLandscape(pts)` and `lineHitsLandscape(pts, hw, bridge)`; only a path with `type:"bridge"` (`isBridge`, `BRIDGE` in data.js) crosses park water. Named modern plants (`PARK_PLANTS` → `LAND["q-<name>"]`, `PARK_PLANTS_OF[biome]`) are Quaternary plants usable anywhere, with `look`/`accent` for drawing (`plantSvg`) and `wet`/`aquatic` for water. Other periods' plants need the ORACLE tech `sterile` to go outside exhibits. Statues (`STATUES` → `LAND["st-<id>"]`, `statue` field) are dev-mode only for v1.0 (`statuesOn()` in data.js = `devMode()`: with it off the Statues dropdown is hidden, `statueOpen` is false so `decorProblem` refuses them, and `grantStatue(g)` is null so grants and the award toast never mention or unlock one; statues already in a save keep loading, drawing and cheering guests) and go outside exhibits only; a few are `free`, the rest unlock with a grant's `statue` field (`statueOpen`, `statueGrant`, shown by `grantPrize`). A statue's plinth uses its theme's path color (`statueSvg`). Guests: `indexDecor(nodes)` (called from `buildGuestGraph`) marks each guest-path node with `n.decor` (0 to 1, `DECOR`) and `n.statues`; `guestsTick` turns decor into mood and the "pretty" thought, and `statueSeen` gives learning (people's statues teach far more). Drawing: `waterSvg`, `featSvg`, `parkWaterSvg`, `decorSvg`, `fenceLineSvg`, `hedgeSvg` (hedge rows, `BARRIERS.hedge`, are drawn as real hedges in exhibits too).
- `js/themes.js`: themes (`THEMES`, `THEME` in data.js).  Zones can carry a `theme` (`zoneThemePlan`, `restyleZone`, `themedZoneAt`): it tints the map under the zone (`THEMES[x].ground`), new builds inside use it, and a panel button restyles what is already there (UI in logiui.js `zoneThemeHtml`). Genesis (the default, no `theme` field) is a full theme too: a conservation campus of forest green, matte gold and warm concrete. `roofs` sort buildings by job (green standing seam for public ones, pale flat lab roofs with `units` for ORACLE/GHOST/TAR/CERES/PMC, dark slate for utilities), exhibits get the steel rail or panel wall with amber post lights while an electric fence is powered (map.js). Paths, buildings and exhibits carry an optional `theme` (none means Genesis) that recolors them (`themeOf`, `themeFill`). `state.themes` holds the build `brush` and the unlocked `have` list; `checkThemes` (called from `checkGoals`) unlocks them by play (Gilded Age by the `gilded` ORACLE tech, Bayou by keeping a water-loving animal, Volcanic by a 4-star rating). `themeZone(e)` checks that the guest paths and shops and restrooms within `THEME.radius` of an exhibit all match it (non-Genesis only), which adds appeal in `recompute`, and `themeFitShare` (species in `THEMES[x].fits`) lifts happiness in `exhibitReport`. Changing a theme costs `fee` of the item's price (`themeFee`, `themeProblem`); new builds use the brush (`themeNew`). The pickers are in ui.js (`themeHtml`, `themesOverview`). Loads after landscape.js.
- `js/biomepaint.js`: paintable biome ground outside exhibits, loaded just before map.js. **Ground tiles:** a biome listed in `GROUND_TILES` (data.js: `{plain, detail, share}`) has 8 x 8 px, 1 m tiles, `sprites/ground/<biome>-<n>.png` (8 px a meter, so 0.125 m a pixel, like the 18 m `<biome>.png` tiles): the first `plain` are plain ground, the next `detail` carry a mark. `buildGroundTiles` (end of biomepaint.js) composes a 100 m square with one tile per 1 m cell: in each 5 m chunk about `share` of the cells (the lowest hashes, `groundPicks`) get a detail tile, the rest plain, and each cell is turned and mirrored at random by `cellHash` (except tiles in `still`, like the lit stone), so it is fixed between draws and reloads. The result goes into both the `#p-<biome>` (park ground, paint outside exhibits) and `#b-<biome>` (exhibit floors) patterns' images. `state.biomePaint` is a list of `{id, biome, r:[x0,y0,x1,y1]}` patches on the 1 m grid, drawn in list order by `paintSvg` (cached by `paintVer`; `#p-<biome>` pattern over the biome's `park` color, clipped to `#plotClip`) in `render` just above the park ground and under paths, buildings and exhibits. It is decoration only; `state.biome` stays locked. `paintRect(biome, box)` deletes patches the new one fully covers, trims partly covered ones when one rectangle is left, merges same-biome neighbours when nothing above them overlaps, and ignores a repaint of what is already on top; `eraseRect` splits patches, `erasePaintAt`, `repickPaint(id, biome)` (tap a patch with the tool). The `paint` tool (Landscaping > Paint Ground; `paintBar`, `endPaintDrag`, `pdrag`, `paintPick` biome or "erase" in map.js, biome buttons in `#dSizes`) drags a box in whole 5 m chunks like the path tools (`pdrag` holds chunk coords, `paintBox(a, b)` turns two chunks into meters, `paintBoxW` does it from world points; a tap paints or erases one chunk; old patches keep their odd sizes). `endPaintDrag` also cuts water out of the box with `cutWaterAll` after a paint that went through, or an erase (even where there was no paint); `paintDragStat` and the toast show the water removed and the refund. The cut lives in `endPaintDrag`, not `paintRect`/`eraseRect`, so Bulldoze's paint erase leaves water alone; Bulldoze on bare ground also takes paint off (`bulldozePaint`). `upgradeSave` defaults the list. Inside open exhibits (not vivariums) the same paint is drawn over the floor (`exhibitPaintSvg`, clipped to the exhibit) and decides the exhibit's biome: `biomeOf(e)` (landscape.js) is `paintedBiome(e)`, the biome with the most sampled ground (`paintShare`, cached per exhibit), where unpainted ground counts as `e.biome` and a tie keeps it. Painting inside exhibits costs `exhibitPaintCost` (the biome's `perSqM` for each m² not already that biome); out in the park it's free. The exhibit panel's Biome section (`biomeHtml`) shows the split and a Paint ground button; only vivariums still have the old Ground dropdown (`regradeProblem`/`regradeCost`).
