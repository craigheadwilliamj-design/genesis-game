# Paths rework: cell-based paths (sessions P1 to P6)

Decisions: every path is 5 m wide (no wide path; lay paths side by side and they merge into one big path). Paths run in straight or 45 degree strokes only. Tram track is not part of this (dev-only, stays as lines). Old saves: polylines are rasterized into cells in `upgradeSave`.

Model: a path is a set of 1 m grid cells, `state.pathCells` keyed `"x,y"`, value a kind (`g` guest, `s` staff, `b` bridge). A drag lays a 5 m wide swath along a straight or 45 degree run, like the fence drag (`pieceLine`, `planPieces`, `layPieces` in js/fences.js are the model). Touching cells are connected; reach is a flood fill from the entrance. Rendering is one ground layer under everything, textured in world space.

Run in order. P1 first; P2 to P5 each need P1; P4 and P5 can run in either order after P2/P3; P6 last.

## P1: Data model, laying, ghost, removal, migration
Add `state.pathCells`, helpers (`cellKey`, `pathKind(x,y)`, `pathAt`), and a drag-and-drop path tool replacing the point-by-point one in js/map.js (tools `path`, `service`, `bridge`; remove the `wide` tool and `WIDE_PATH` from the menu): drag lays a 5 m swath along a straight or 45 degree run; the ghost shows exactly the cells that will be laid (true size), green/red per cell with the reason; releasing buys it. Cost per cell from the old per-meter rates divided by 5 (guest, staff, bridge). Placement checks per cell: owned land only (`inOwned`), not through exhibits/fence/buildings/water (bridge crosses water only), keep clear of the entrance gate but allow paths to start at it. Bulldoze removes cells (drag or tap). Keep the old `state.paths` working in parallel for now: migrate in `upgradeSave` by rasterizing each old path's centerline with its `halfWidth` into cells and clearing `state.paths` (tram paths stay in `state.paths`). Temporary rendering: flat colored cells per kind (the real texture is P4). Everything that reads `state.paths` for non-tram paths must keep working through a compatibility layer until P2/P3 replace it (e.g. a derived list of strips), so the game stays playable after P1. Update CLAUDE.md. Smoke checks: drag lays the right cells, 45 degree drags, bulldoze, migration of an old save, cost.

## P2: Connectivity
Joined/reach from cells: `derived.joined`/`joinedAll` (flood fill from the entrance cells, guest and bridge cells for guests; guest+staff for staff reach), edge-to-edge only (4-connected, not diagonal corners unless a full cell touches). Staff vs guest rules: staff cells and guest cells touching are separate networks and stay clean (no connection) unless a gate/crossing marker is placed (add a small `Crossing` tile tool) which joins them for staff only; guests never use staff cells. Exhibit gates, buildings, depots and path props "touch a path" checks (`near(e.gate, ...)`, `isReachable`, service attach) move to cells. Replace the compatibility layer from P1 where it relates to reach. Smoke checks for each rule.

## P3: Guest and keeper navigation on cells
Rebuild `buildGuestGraph` (js/guests.js) and `buildKeeperGraph` (js/keepers.js) on cells: guests walk anywhere on guest/bridge cells (any position across the 5 m width, cross over, spread along exhibit fences and platforms), never step on staff or non-path cells, pathfinding stays on cells, stops (`guestField`, food stands, restrooms, exhibits) attach to the nearest path cell edge. Crowding (`GUEST.crowd`, `node.room`) comes from the local path width (cells across), so laying paths side by side raises capacity (replaces the wide-path doubling). Keep performance fine with up to 1200 parties: route on a coarser graph over the cells (e.g. 2 to 3 m nodes) and let walkers wander within the mask. Keepers/vets/mechanics/custodians/guards use staff cells (and guest cells where allowed today). Remove any remaining reads of old `state.paths` for non-tram paths. Smoke checks: guests never on staff cells, cross a path, spread at an exhibit, reach every stop.

## P4: Rendering and layering
One ground layer for all path cells, drawn below props, plants, buildings, animals, platforms and fences (only bridges above water). World-aligned pixel texture per kind (guest paving, staff gravel/asphalt, bridge planks) that tiles without seams, with edge bevels cut to the pixel grid from the cell mask (like `waterMask` in js/landscape.js), correct at T-junctions, mergers, 45 degree runs and diagonal merges, and a clean edge where guest and staff paths meet. Cheap: cache the rendered mask/canvas and only redraw when cells change. Follow the art direction in CLAUDE.md (flat, light top left). Smoke check: layer order and no overdraw of props.

## P5: Placement rules everywhere
Move all `lineDist(...p.points)`, `linesCross`, `lineShapeDist`, `lineEntersShape` path checks to cells: exhibits (`exhibitProblem`), fences (`pieceSpotProblem`), buildings, landscape (`decorProblem`, `landSpot`, `waterProblem`), viewing platforms, path objects (bins, benches, signs touch a path edge), gates, camera/quiet-sign reach, and the Park Map. Fix the prop-vs-path clipping from the original roadmap. Smoke checks.

## P6: Polish and performance
Profile the cell flood fill, graph rebuilds and render with a big park; incremental updates instead of full rebuilds on each drag; undo-friendly bulldoze; tool hints; tidy dead code (old point-by-point path code, `WIDE_PATH`, compatibility layer); update CLAUDE.md fully.


## Revision (after P1 landed): chunks, drag-bulldoze, built-in crossings
- A path is a 5 m x 5 m CHUNK, not 1 m cells. Chunk coordinates are `floor(x / 5)`; `state.pathCells` becomes `state.pathChunks` (same kinds g/s/b). A drag lays whole chunks along a straight or 45 degree run (diagonal runs fill the extra chunks needed to stay edge-connected, a 2-wide stair step). Side by side chunks merge into a bigger path. All placement/ghost/bulldoze snap to the chunk grid.
- Bulldoze is a drag-box: with the Bulldoze tool, press and drag across paths; the chunks inside the box are highlighted as you drag; releasing deletes them (one refund, one undo-less confirm only if it is large). A tap on a single chunk deletes just that chunk.
- No Crossing tile. A staff path chunk that touches a guest/bridge chunk (or a building door, exhibit gate, depot) is automatically connected for STAFF; guests still never use staff chunks. Staff paths touching guest paths stay visually distinct (clean edge).
- New session P1b (chunk conversion + drag-bulldoze + built-in crossings) runs after P2 and before P3/P4/P5, converting the cell code, the migration and P2's flood fill to chunks and removing the Crossing tile. P3, P4 and P5 are then written against chunks.
