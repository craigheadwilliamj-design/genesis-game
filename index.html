Welcome to Genesis Park

You have $150,000 and an empty plot. Turn it into a park people will pay to see.

  1. Draw an exhibit from Build, Exhibit Tools, Fence Types. Pick a fence, tap corners on the map, then tap the first corner to close it.
  2. Connect it with a path that starts from the entrance at the bottom. Guests only see exhibits beside a path.
  3. Buy starter animals by tapping the exhibit. Give them enough room and the group size they like.
  4. Add food stands and restrooms so guests stay happy and spend more.
  5. Bring back the rest from the past. ORACLE unlocks animals, GHOST travels back for their DNA, TAR clones them, and CERES grows plants and medicine. It all takes time.

Each day lasts about a minute. Guests pay at the gate, and you pay for food, wages, and upkeep when the park closes. The side panel always shows your next goal.
